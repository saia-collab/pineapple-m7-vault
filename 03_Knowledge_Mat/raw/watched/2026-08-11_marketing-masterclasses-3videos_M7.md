# 🎬 3 Marketing Masterclasses → Pineapple Roofing
**What the pros teach, rewritten for a Frisco/DFW roofing company. Nothing here publishes — drafts land PAUSED.**

These three videos say the same thing from three angles: **a good offer + a simple repeatable system beats a pile of random ads.** Here's how each maps to roofing.

## 🧠 THE 3 BIG IDEAS (one per video)

### 1. Hormozi "Scale or Fail" → **the Referral + Event engine**
A concierge doctor was stuck because he only had word-of-mouth. Hormozi's fixes translate straight to roofing:
- **"Bam-fam" (book-a-job-from-a-job):** at every touchpoint, set the next step *and ask who else needs it.* → **Roofing version:** after every inspection or job, ask *"which neighbor's roof should we photo-audit while we're on your street?"* Storms hit whole blocks — one job should spawn three CPPAs next door.
- **The giveaway offer:** give away one big-ticket thing (a full year of service). Everyone who enters gets *something*; no sticker shock because everyone hears the price. → **Roofing version:** a quarterly **"Full Roof Restoration Giveaway"** to your neighborhood/past-customer list — every entrant gets a **Complimentary Professional Photo Audit (CPPA)**. Know your math: one comped roof vs. dozens of inspections booked.
- **Event-based marketing beats pure ads for high-trust sales:** bring the "machine," give a free scan, sell while they wait. → **Roofing version:** HOA / neighborhood **"Storm Check" events** after a hail event — free drone roof scans on the spot, brand video playing while you scan, book the CPPA follow-up before they leave.
- **Newsletter P.S. CTA:** low-pressure ask at the bottom of every email. → Add a one-line P.S. to every M7 email: *"P.S. This quarter we're giving away a full roof restoration — every neighbor who enters gets a free photo audit."* (free→CPPA in customer-facing copy).

### 2. Meta Ads Master Course → **systems/funnels, not piles of ads**
The Hyros CEO sees billions in ad spend. What actually wins:
- **Each campaign = ONE funnel for ONE buyer, 5–8 ads that work *together*** to walk a person from top → bottom. **Judge the funnel's ROI, not each ad.**
- **Fewer ads win.** Don't dump 50 creatives; find the best *hook* and let the funnel do the work. "Ad fatigue" is mostly a myth if the funnel is built right.
- **A landing page per buyer type**, built fast with Claude/AI. Simple + clear beats pretty.
- **Creative testing = sniper tweaks** of a *winning* ad (same pitch, new angle), not mass generation.
- **The cookie-cutter funnel** (use this exact order): **① UGC hook** (educate on the problem) → **② problem + how you fix it** → **③ straight offer** → **④ client result / testimonial** → **⑤ objection-handling offer.** Mix video + static.

**🍍 Roofing translation — build ONE funnel per buyer:**
| Buyer | Best hook (top of funnel) | Their landing page is about… |
|---|---|---|
| Storm/hail homeowner | "Hail hit Frisco — here's how to know if *your* roof is damaged (most people miss it)" | free damage check → CPPA |
| Old-roof homeowner | "If your roof is 15+ years old, watch this before your next storm" | roof-age → replacement urgency |
| New-build / realtor | "Buying/selling in Frisco? Get the roof documented first" | inspection + documentation |

The 5-ad sequence for each: **UGC roof-tip → how our CPPA works → the offer → a real Frisco job result → "worried about cost/insurance?" objection ad.**

### 3. Hormozi 1-Hour Ads → **content IS ads + do more (volume)**
- **Content and ads have merged.** Post lots of content, let the algorithm reveal the winners, then **run the winners as ads** with a simple banner CTA ("click to book your free photo audit"). Take **organic winners → ads**, not the reverse.
- **Run your best *saves*, not your best *views*.** The reels people *save* (roof tips, "signs you need a new roof") are your best ad candidates.
- **Message = the prospect.** Show the customer you want (a Frisco homeowner, a real DFW roof, your Polynesian family crew) and that targeting takes care of itself.
- **Rule of 100:** pick ONE and do it daily — 100 outreaches, OR 100 minutes making content (+post daily), OR 100 minutes making paid ads. Consistency + volume is the whole game.
- **Position vs. the "leader":** you're not fighting the giant agency roofers head-on — you win on the true, un-arguable angles: **RCAT #03-0637, IKO Certified, Polynesian family-owned since 2005, 5-star, local Frisco crew.** Weave those *facts* into every ad.
- **"Best comment" free ads:** leave the most helpful comment on local Frisco/Nextdoor-style posts about storms/roofs — free attention.
- **Proof first:** nobody believes you can do it until you *show* it — lead with job photos, drone scans, before/after.
