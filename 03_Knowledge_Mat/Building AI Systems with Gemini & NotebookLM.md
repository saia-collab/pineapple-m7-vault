# SOP: How To Use Gemini Gems + NotebookLM To Build Accurate AI Business Systems

**Purpose:** This SOP explains how to combine Gemini Gems with NotebookLM to create AI systems that are both accurate and written in your brand voice.

The core architectural concept is simple: **NotebookLM holds your facts (Ground Truth), while Gemini Gems hold your style, tone, and execution rules.** This configuration eliminates the need to choose between accurate but robotic writing, or good-sounding writing that hallucinates.

---

## 🛠️ Part 1: Architecture Overview

### What NotebookLM Does
NotebookLM acts as your source-grounded knowledge vault. It reads your raw assets—including PDFs, Google Docs, spreadsheets, websites, YouTube videos, and audio files—and answers questions using *only* those parameters. Answers are strictly linked back to citations in the source text to prevent structural hallucination.

### What Gemini Gems Do
Gemini Gems serve as your custom, persistent AI system sub-agents. You configure them once with specific instructions on persona, tone, writing style, formatting, and task structures. Because their operational boundaries are locked in, they do not suffer from the "context drift" found in normal chat loops.

> 💡 **The Simple Rule:** Use NotebookLM for facts. Use Gemini Gems for style and execution.

---

## 🦾 Part 2: Building a Gemini Gem (The PACT Framework)

To build a reusable, single-task business sub-agent, navigate to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com), open **Gems**, and configure your new Gem using the **PACT Framework**:

*   **P - Persona:** Define the explicit identity and voice of the agent.
    *   *Example:* "You are a direct, premium B2B copywriter who writes concise, authoritative documentation and deletes any words that do not add value."
*   **A - Assignment:** Give the Gem exactly *one* repeatable objective. Do not combine multiple unrelated jobs into a single Gem.
    *   *Example:* "Write short follow-up notes to prospective clients who went quiet after receiving an audit evaluation."
*   **C - Context:** Define the strict constraints, guardrails, and compliance laws.
    *   *Example:* "Keep every note under 100 words. Never use low-ticket buzzwords, sales slop, or generic industry clichés."
*   **T - Template:** Provide the exact layout parameters the AI must output.
    *   *Example:* "Structure outputs strictly in this order: Subject line, Greeting, 2-sentence Body, single clear Next Step CTA."

    *   `/new`: Flushes current chat context space completely when switching to a completely fresh project task.
    *   `/compact`: Compresses a long task thread, saving a clean structural summary to your states log while discarding redundant intermediate conversational tokens.

### 2. Network Security Architecture (Tailscale / Cloudflare Tunnel)
Never expose your Hermes Agent OS dashboard or VPS root ports to the public internet without an authentication wrap. Protect the engine using one of these two verified security paths:

```text
[Public Mobile Device / Laptop] 
              |
      (Secure Auth Layer) ---> [Cloudflare Tunnel / Tailscale VPN] 
                                            |
                                  [Private Port Routing]
                                            |
                                 [Hermes 24/7 VPS Terminal]
                                            |
                                    [Obsidian Vault]
```

*   **Path A: Cloudflare Tunnel:** Map a secure tunnel route from your domain host directly to the local port running Hermes inside the VPS. This exposes the dashboard behind an isolated, encrypted SSL login page without opening direct port vulnerabilities.
*   **Path B: Tailscale Private Mesh:** Install Tailscale on both your personal mobile devices/laptops and your VPS. This creates an isolated, encrypted virtual private network (VPN). The Hermes terminal remains completely invisible to the outside internet, accessible only when your device is securely logged into your private network mesh.
