**Step 5: Run the Google Flow AI Video Factory Pipeline**

Convert your grounded research documentation or written blog drafts into hyper-engaging video assets. Run this streamlined workflow directly inside your unified dashboard interface:

\[ GDrive AutoSync Data \] ──\> \[ Plan & Script \] ──\> \[ Batch Scene Generation \] ──\> \[ Brand Style Edit \] ──\> \[ Organize & Review \]

### **1\. Initialize and Activate the Agent**

Open your dashboard's Google Flow module on your computer workspace and explicitly engage the agent:

**Action Prompt:** Turn on Google Flow Agent inside this project so it can help me plan, create, edit, and organize my video.

### **2\. Grounded Brainstorming & Scripting**

Instruct the agent to look at your active research files or specified blog outlines to write custom multimedia dialogue:

**Action Prompt:** Help me plan a 30-second promotional video about the topic found in our active research note. Give me a clear scene-by-scene outline, then write a conversational, human script for the host.

### **3\. Batch Scene Generation with Style Anchors**

Instead of creating clips one by one, force the agent to generate multiple alternate variations simultaneously to give you the best structural options:

**Action Prompt:** Create 5 different opening shot options for this script. Review the variations, tell me which one is strongest for our conversion goal, and build the subsequent scenes using identical visual style and character consistency parameters.

### **4\. Enforce Asset and Character Continuity**

To ensure your visual avatars do not look completely randomized across scene transitions, use strict @ mention reference variables:

**Action Prompt:** Use @host in this scene and keep the same face, outfit, and visual environment style parameters.

### **5\. Batch Editing & Brand Styling**

Do not waste hours editing individual frames or adjustments manually. Use targeted batch filters to enforce visual styling:

**Action Prompt:** Apply a warm, friendly color style to all clips in Scene 3 and every clip longer than 5 seconds.

### **6\. Automated Asset Cleanup & Project Organization**

Command the agent to keep your workspace structure tidy:

**Action Prompt:** Rename these messy clips so I know what each one is, group them by scene, and move unused assets away into an archive collection.

## ---

**Step 7: Implement the Hermes Loop Engineering Framework**

Instead of manually checking compiled assets, deploy your autonomous **Builder-Judge feedback loop** to automatically review content, code, and video tracking parameters.

\[Define Done\] ──\> \[Builder Agent Generates\] ──\> \[Judge Agent (Opus 4.8) Scores\] ──\> \[Fix Loop / Under 90\] ──\> \[Pass / Save to Obsidian\]

1. **Define "Done" Clearly:** Write precise rules for execution (e.g., *"The compiled article must target the designated keyword cluster, contain exactly 4 internal links, feature correct H2/H3 hierarchy, and include our newly generated Google Flow video embed"*).  
2. **Builder Generates:** The Builder Agent (Hermes/Kimi/GLM) creates the initial draft.  
3. **Verification Agent Evaluation:** An isolated **Verification Agent** running on Claude Opus 4.8 evaluates the output. It checks for brand tone consistency, formatting compliance, and optimization parameters, then scores it on a scale of 1 to 100\.  
4. **Self-Correction Loop:** If the score falls below 90, the system automatically routes the asset back down the chain with granular optimization instructions. The loop continues autonomously until it satisfies your threshold or hits a pre-configured 5-round limit.
