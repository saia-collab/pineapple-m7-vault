# Storm Damage Estimator Manual (Frisco / North Texas) [p. 1]

## 1. Pre-storm baseline

- Capture pre-storm roof condition with date-stamped drone photos [p. 3]
- Confirm RCAT licensing status before climbing any roof [p. 4]
- Document existing flashing condition at every penetration [p. 5]

## 2. Hail assessment (75033 / 75034 / 75035)

Hail Hit: A bruise or fracture on a shingle caused by impact, typically 1.0"+ diameter.
- Bruising > 0.5 inch diameter on 8+ shingles per slope = Full Restoration Coverage candidate
- Soft metal verification (vents, gutters) confirms hail fall size [p. 7]
- Photo grid: 4 corners + ridge + each penetration, per slope [p. 8]

Wind Damage: Creased or missing tabs from straight-line winds.
- Creasing on 6+ tabs per slope = Full Restoration Coverage candidate
- Direction-of-debris analysis helps prove wind vs. mechanical damage [p. 9]

## 3. Insurance claim discipline (Texas)

- Texas hail-claim window: typically 12 months from date of loss [p. 11]
- Document within 30 days of a significant storm for cleanest evidence [p. 12]
- Suit-filing deadline: 2 years from date of loss [p. 13]

## 4. Material specifications (IKO only)

- Class 4 IR shingle upgrade available for impact-resistant reroofs
- IKO manufacturer warranty requires RCAT-licensed installer for full coverage
- Synthetic underlayment required on every re-deck

## 5. Workflow (no ZIP — verify applicability)

- Schedule CPPA within 48 hours of lead capture
- Walk adjuster through photo record line by line
- Submit final documentation packet within 14 days of CPPA
