# 🎯 M7 DESIGN BENCHMARK — copy Neal's STRUCTURE, keep OUR brand law

> Standing rule: whenever we build a page, check `nealrfg.com` for format. **Adopt their structure — NOT
> their words.** Neal uses "Free Estimate"; we NEVER do (brand law → CPPA / Complimentary).

## 🚩 URGENT FIX FIRST — kill "FREE" on the live site (your brother's catch)
Your live `pineappleroofingllc.com` still says **"Get your FREE Quote Today" / "Get A FREE Quote."** That
breaks M7 law and weakens the brand. Fix it in WordPress today:

**Fastest (site-wide, WordPress):** install the free plugin **"Better Search Replace"** → run these exact
swaps (tick **"Run as dry run" first**, review, then run for real):
| Find | Replace with |
|------|-------------|
| `FREE Quote` | `Complimentary Quote` |
| `Free Quote` | `Complimentary Quote` |
| `Free Estimate` | `Complimentary Estimate` |
| `free inspection` | `Complimentary Professional Photo Audit` |
| `Get a Free` | `Get a Complimentary` |

**Per-page (Elementor):** Home → **Edit with Elementor** → click the hero form heading ("Get your FREE Quote
Today"), the yellow **"Get A FREE Quote"** button, and the top-nav **"Get a Free Quote"** button → change each
to **"Get Your Complimentary Quote"** (or **"Reserve My Roof Audit"**). Update the form's fine print too.
Then check the live site + re-run your brand_firewall mindset: zero "free," zero green.

> Even better positioning: swap the offer itself to **"Reserve Your Complimentary Professional Photo Audit"** —
> it out-classes "free quote" and matches the capture page + ads.

## 🧱 WHAT NEAL DOES WELL (steal these moves)
1. **One page per city (location pages)** — `/locations/west-palm-beach-fl`, boca-raton, delray, jupiter…
   This is the SEO engine. **We do the same: a page each for Frisco, Lewisville, Plano, McKinney, Allen, The Colony.**
2. **Trust band, repeated** — "4.9★ RATED BY 400+ CUSTOMERS" + "50-YEAR WARRANTY" with laurel graphics, dropped
   ~5 times down the page. **We repeat: "5.0★ · 200+ reviews · License #03-0637 · IKO · 50-yr warranty."**
3. **"As seen on" authority row** — press logos + award badge. **We use: minority-owned (NMSDC), BBB, IKO,
   5.0★, "since 2005."**
4. **Video testimonials** (YouTube embeds) + a big claim ("99.9% satisfaction"). **We use our reels +
   before/after clips + real Google reviews.**
5. **Premium positioning, not discount** — *"we're more expensive for good reason."* **This is the whole point:
   complimentary audit, premium install — never compete on "free/cheap."**
6. **Founder/family story** — childhood friends, family team, "your home is a sanctuary." **This is Pineapple's
   superpower: Polynesian-owned, family-operated, since 2005, Tauhi Vā. Tell it bigger.**
7. **Service cards w/ photos → dedicated service pages** ("Explore service →").
8. **Clean contact block** — phone + email + map + license + Google-reviews badge + social. Simple.
