# The Seven-Day Digital Growth and SEO Execution Protocol

- [ ] **Monday: Performance Audit & GSC Keyword Ingestion**
  - **Weekly Routine:** Extract Google Search Console (GSC) performance metrics covering the last 90 days. Isolate easy-win "striking-distance" keywords ranking between average positions 5.0 and 20.0 with high impressions (> 250/month) and low CTR (< 3.0%) [1, 2]. Ingest fresh storm and SERP data into the project's Gemini Notebook [3].
  - **Required Dashboard Tab:** `OpenSEO → Search Performance` / `Notebook` [4, 5].
  - **Required Validation Script:** `run-monday-keywords.bat` and `gsc_striking_distance.py` [6, 7].

- [ ] **Tuesday: Creative Planning & Field Asset Uploads**
  - **Weekly Routine:** Organize your creative asset plan, review newly synced OMI voice notes, and extract client-specific proof points [3, 4]. Upload high-resolution job site photography from local North Texas projects directly to Google Business Profile (GBP) and the local staging folder [3, 4].
  - **Required Dashboard Tab:** `Blotato` / `Outbox_Drafts` [5].
  - **Required Validation Script:** `brand_firewall.py --check` on draft campaigns and social graphics [8].

- [ ] **Wednesday: Automated Review Campaigns & Video Compiles**
  - **Weekly Routine:** Text or email yesterday's completed-job customers a review link to accelerate regional map pack authority [4, 9]. Trigger the content generation engine to render vertical video clips and compile landing pages with zero cuts to hook local storm victims [3, 10].
  - **Required Dashboard Tab:** `Execute` / `Studio` [5, 11].
  - **Required Validation Script:** `brand_firewall.py --check` to automatically scan generated assets [12].

- [ ] **Thursday: Quality Assurance & Review Interaction**
  - **Weekly Routine:** Run manual checks on the Outbox folder. Individually reply to new Google reviews by explicitly calling out the client's neighborhood to boost geolocation power, and publish one GBP update post [3, 4].
  - **Required Dashboard Tab:** `Pipeline` / `WordPress` / `Blotato` [5, 11].
  - **Required Validation Script:** Manual review and approval of PAUSED drafts in `01_Command_Center/Outbox_Drafts/` [8].

- [ ] **Friday: Speed-to-Lead Audits & GSC Inspection**
  - **Weekly Routine:** Run a lead speed-to-lead qualification sweep to confirm that every new inbound lead from Google Local Services Ads (LSA) and Meta Ads was contacted or called within 5 minutes [9, 13]. Scan GSC for newly indexed pages and impression changes [4, 13].
  - **Required Dashboard Tab:** `OpenSEO` / `LSA Dashboard` [5, 13].
  - **Required Validation Script:** `GSC_Connect.bat` [5].

- [ ] **Saturday: Video Distribution & Performance Scoreboard**
  - **Weekly Routine:** Publish one short-form vertical video reel across connected platforms according to your social media Repurpose Plan [3, 4]. Review your overall keyword rankings and audit ad campaign CTR metrics [4, 13].
  - **Required Dashboard Tab:** `Video Editor` / `Blotato` / `Mission Control` [5].
  - **Required Validation Script:** `LAUNCH_ALL.bat` and `M7_DOCTOR.bat` [6, 14].

- [ ] **Sunday: Long-Term Memory Sync & Repository Backups**
  - **Weekly Routine:** Run deep housecleaning across your workspace. Sync OMI voice transcripts and log completed campaign metrics into your shared brain file so your agents never suffer from amnesia [3, 4, 15]. Backup your workspace to your private GitHub repository [16, 17].
  - **Required Dashboard Tab:** `Shared Memory` / `Pipeline` [11].
  - **Required Validation Script:** `M7_CLEANUP.bat` and `git push` [4, 6].
