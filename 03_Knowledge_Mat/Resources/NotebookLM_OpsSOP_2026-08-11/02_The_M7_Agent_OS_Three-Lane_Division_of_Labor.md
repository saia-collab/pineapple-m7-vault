# The M7 Agent OS Three-Lane Division of Labor

The **Three-Lane Division of Labor** is the structural core of the M7 Agent OS, designed specifically to run your roofing and restoration businesses with zero waste and maximum brand security [1, 2]. By routing every task to the cheapest, most specialized model in your stack, you can replace expensive agency services (like Scorpion) for $0 in monthly software fees [3, 4].

The workload is divided into three distinct lanes:

### 1. The BUILD Lane (Technical & Automation Engine)
*   **Assigned Agent:** **Codex / GPT 5.6 (running unmetered via ChatGPT OAuth)** [1].
*   **What it owns:** Python scripts, schema markup, WordPress automated posting integrations (via `wp_publish.py`), custom command-center dashboards, and deep technical-SEO implementations [1, 5, 6].
*   **Why this works:** Because Codex is completely **unmetered** under your upgraded Plus plan, you can point this agent directly at your entire local workspace vault and file directory [1, 7]. It can write, debug, and configure your local code 24/7 without worrying about API key usage or token limits [1, 7].

### 2. The ORCHESTRATE + VOICE Lane (Brand & Compliance Guard)
*   **Assigned Agent:** **Claude Code** [1].
*   **What it owns:** Brand-voice copywriting, elite landing page drafting, social media captions, review replies, workflow planning, and directory structure management [1].
*   **Why this works:** Claude Code knows **M7 brand law cold** [1]. Volume-oriented models are prone to hallucinating, but Claude is your high-priority seat designed to strictly enforce your core brand guardrails before any file is staged [1, 8, 9]:
    *   **Brand Lexicon:** Enforces that you never write "free" (always **CPPA** / Complimentary Professional Photo Audit), never write "$0 down" (always **Full Restoration Coverage**), and never say IKO Certified (always **IKO Certified** to honor your credentials) [9].
    *   **Visual Guidelines:** Programmatically blocks any "green" from entering your system, maintaining your strict palette of Royal Navy #1A365D, Pineapple Gold #FBC02D, and Status Cyan #00BFFF [9].
    *   **Identity Anchors:** Verifies that your official business coordinates are always present and accurate: *Polynesian-owned, RCAT #03-0637, IKO Certified, since 2005, 972-928-0788* [9].

### 3. The RESEARCH / IDEATE Lane (Creative Spark)
*   **Assigned Agent:** **ChatGPT (Plus, web-connected)** [1].
*   **What it owns:** Keyword brainstorming, local roofing competitor sweeps, and creating the initial structural angles for your campaigns [1].
*   **Why this works:** This lane is optimized for rapid, conversational research [1]. Because ChatGPT has live web access, it can quickly spot what regional competitors are doing [1]. Because its raw outputs do not natively respect your strict brand lexicon, you never publish its drafts directly—instead, you feed ChatGPT's raw creative research back to Claude Code or Codex to be properly firewalled, formatted, and finalized [1].

### Integrating the Lanes with Your Daily Operations
To keep your operating costs at absolute zero, you scale this three-lane system with free local resources:
*   **The Bulk Workhorse:** For high-volume content, you rotate your unmetered lanes with **Hermes (Goal Mode)** running overnight to generate bulk city landing pages, blog drafts, and Google Business Profile updates [5, 10].
*   **The Outbox Shield:** No matter which lane or agent drafts a file, everything is generated in a **PAUSED** state and dropped into `01_Command_Center/Outbox_Drafts/` [9, 11]. No agent is ever allowed to post, publish, or spend ad budget autonomously [9, 11]. You (Saia) are the only physical publisher [11].
*   **The Safety Gate:** Before any draft is cleared for your review, it must be run through `python 04_Tech_Lab/scripts/brand_firewall.py --check` to programmatically guarantee compliance [1, 9].
