### 🎬 How We Use It: Building 3D Scrolling "Alive" Websites
One of the highest-leverage applications for this integration is building modern, award-grade landing pages [7, 8]. In standard web templates, everything is static [8]. On a luxury page, **products rotate or change dynamically as the visitor scrolls** [8, 9]. 

With Higgsfield and your unmetered coding agents, you can build these scrolling effects in an afternoon [10, 11]:
* **The "One-Take" Secret:** These scrolling animations are not actual 3D objects; they are high-resolution, unbroken video clips whose playback speed and direction are mapped directly to the visitor's scroll position [12, 13]. Scroll down, and the video scrubs forward; scroll up, and it runs in reverse [12].
* **Generating the Take:** We use **Seedance 2.5** to render a single, flawless 30-second take with no visual camera cuts [11, 13]. A cut would shatter the scroll illusion [13]. For example, you can prompt: 
  * *"Generate a 30-second single unbroken shot of our IKO Certified shingle, slowly rotating on a dark surface, macro close-up, soft golden light traveling across the granules, static camera, zero cuts [14, 15]."*
* **Wiring the Scroll:** Once the video is rendered, your coding agent builds the HTML and CSS to wire the video frames to the scroll bar [11, 16]. 

A prime example of this in action is the **Abyss Mark II** watch campaign, where scrolling down scrubs a video of a titanium watch smoothly descending from turquoise surface waters into absolute ocean darkness [7, 17, 18].
