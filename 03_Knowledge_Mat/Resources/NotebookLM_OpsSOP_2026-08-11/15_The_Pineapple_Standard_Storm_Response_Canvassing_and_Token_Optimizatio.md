### 💸 How Token Optimization Layers (Like Headroom) Save Money

In an Agent OS, your software costs are driven heavily by **API tokens** (the basic units of data AI models use to read and write) [10]. A token optimization layer like **Headroom** acts as an intelligent compression barrier between your workspace files and your AI agents to keep your bills near zero [11].

Here is how Headroom saves you money and improves performance:

*   **Massive Cost Reductions:** Headroom is designed to cut token consumption by **50% to 95%** on the exact same tasks [11]. In real-world benchmarks, it regularly reduces token usage by **20% to 30%** depending on the complexity of your memory system and the model being used [11].
*   **Trimming the Noise:** AI models often waste tokens rereading redundant, repetitive data in large system prompts or vault folders [12]. Headroom strips away the "fluff" and compresses the context down to what actually matters before handing the task to the model [12].
*   **Sharper, Faster Answers:** Shrinking the context doesn't make the AI's answer worse—it actually makes it **sharper and more accurate** [12]. When a prompt is bloated with background noise, the agent can get lost in the context [13]. Trimming the prompt allows the model to focus its limited "attention" entirely on solving your core problem rather than wading through irrelevant details [12].
*   **Preserving Rate Limits & Headroom:** Standard API plans have strict monthly or hourly limits [10]. By processing your local files through a compression layer, you preserve your unmetered "headroom," run more background loops autonomously, and avoid hitting rate-limit walls mid-build [10, 14, 15].
