### Topic 2: Understanding the "50% Content Rule"

#### 1) Plain-English "What It Is + Why It Matters for a Frisco Roofer"
The **50% Content Rule** is a strict local SEO requirement stating that at least half of the text on your local suburb pages (like Stonebriar vs. Newman Village) must be completely different, unique, and highly localized.

*   **Jargon Glossary:**
    *   **Crawl Budget:** The limited amount of time and resources search engine bots allocate to reading your website. 
    *   **Content Duplication:** A spam filter penalty triggered when a website copy-pastes the exact same page over and over and only changes the city names.

**Why It Matters to Pineapple Roofing:**
If your new WordPress site has identical pages for both Stonebriar and Newman Village (with only the suburb name swapped out), Google's Helpful Content System will recognize the duplication. It will flag your site as spam, refuse to index (show) the pages, and waste your crawl budget. 

By ensuring **at least 50% unique content** on each page using real neighborhood facts, Google will happily index both, doubling your local search visibility.
