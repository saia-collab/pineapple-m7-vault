**1) Plain-English "What It Is + Why It Matters for a Frisco Roofer"**
A **`claude.md` file** is a single markdown text document placed in your local project folder that acts as a "Shared Memory Brain" for all of your local AI models.

*   *Jargon Gloss:* **Shared Memory Brain** (A master configuration file placed in your local project folder that different local AI models read first before writing any code or copy).
*   *Jargon Gloss:* **Ollama / Hermes** (A free program that lets you run powerful open-source AI models like Hermes directly on your own computer without an internet connection).

**Why it matters for a Frisco roofer:**
When building local suburb pages or replying to customer reviews, you don't want to waste time retyping your company’s state license, manufacturer credentials, and banned vocabulary words over and over. Saving a permanent brand wrapper in your project directory guarantees that every piece of content your local AI generates is automatically formatted with our strict **Navy and Gold** styles, contains your exact state license (**RCAT #03-0637**), and honors your strict brand rules.
