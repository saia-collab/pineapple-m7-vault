# PM7 Agentic OS: The Local Marketing Automation Framework

### 1. Plain-English "What It Is + Why It Matters for a Frisco Roofer"

The **PM7 Agentic OS** is a fully automated local marketing framework that replaces bloated SEO agency fees with a team of eight specialized AI agents [1, 2]. Instead of running random prompts, these agents act as highly trained "digital subcontractors" that collaborate in coordinated waves [1, 3]. They share a single memory base—holding your exact brand facts, certifications, and service terms—to ensure no agent ever hallucinates or writes off-brand filler [3, 4].

*   **Jargon Glossaries for this lesson:**
    *   **MCP (Model Context Protocol):** A standard connection protocol that lets local AI models securely read live internet databases, rankings, and local search trends [5].
    *   **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization):** Restructuring website copy so AI search engines (like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews) pull, cite, and recommend your business [6, 7].
    *   **Content Capsule:** A standalone, high-impact paragraph under an H2/H3 question header that answers a query in exactly 30 to 60 words so AI models can easily copy-paste and cite it [8].
    *   **Schema Markup:** Specialized code placed in a website's header that explicitly tells search engine crawlers who you are, what services you offer, and where you work [9, 10].

**Why It Matters to Pineapple Roofing:**
Traditional SEO is about getting a blue link on Google [6, 11]. Modern AI search is an "answer engine" economy [11]. When a Frisco homeowner asks ChatGPT, *"Who is a licensed, IKO certified roofer near me to look at hail damage?"* the model doesn't just show links [12]. It reviews Page 1 sources [13, 14], looks for machine-readable schemas [10, 15], and extracts direct text [16]. 

With **80% of all AI search citations** originating from sites ranking on Page 1 of Google [17], our Agentic OS guarantees your Frisco-specific services, **RCAT #03-0637** license, and premium **IKO Certified** status are perfectly structured for instant extraction [4, 18].

#### Raw Operational Metrics & Performance Gates:
*   **The 72% Citation Rule:** Exactly **72% of all AI-cited content** uses the Capsule Content structure: a direct answer immediately following a question header [19].
*   **Speed Threshold:** Fully loaded time must be **under 2.5 seconds** [20]. Serves all assets as light **WEBP** images (avoiding performance-killing PNGs) [21, 22].
*   **Agentic Verification:** The site route must host a machine-readable summary file at `/llm.txt` to pass agentic crawl audits [23, 24].
*   **Zero-Publish Protocol:** To manage legal liability, agents operate strictly as draftsmen—review replies, GBP posts, and articles land in queue and **never auto-publish** [4, 25].

#### Step-by-Step Logic for your Terminal Setup:

### 3. Pineapple-Specific Wave Execution Example

Here is how our Wave pipeline collaborates specifically to capture high-intent "Frisco hail storm" leads.

[4, 18]

#### Wave 3: Assembly
