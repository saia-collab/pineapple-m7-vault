# SEO Architecture and Siloing for Local Roofing Services

### 1) Plain-English "What It Is + Why It Matters for a Frisco Roofer"

**Website structure** (or **Siloing**) is organizing your website's pages into a neat, nested folder hierarchy (parent-child relationship) rather than dumping all of your services onto a single, messy homepage [1-3].

*   *Jargon Gloss:* **Siloing / Parent-Child Hierarchy** is arranging your site like folders on your computer—your main "services" folder contains individual "shingle-repair" and "metal-roofs" files, keeping them completely organized instead of loosely scattered [3, 4].
*   *Jargon Gloss:* **Programmatic SEO** is a method for generating unique, local neighborhood landing pages automatically using structured layouts instead of designing each page manually from scratch [5].

**Why it matters to Pineapple Roofing:**
Trying to cram all your services (commercial roofing, storm damage repair, shingle installations) onto a single page is **"SEO suicide"** [1, 6]. Search engines like Google, ChatGPT, and Perplexity do not rank entire websites; they rank **individual pages** that match a user's exact search intent [6, 7]. 

If a Frisco homeowner searches for "hail damage roof repair in Stonebriar," Google will always prioritize a dedicated, hyper-focused page over a generic homepage [6, 8]. A clean, siloed structure ensures every local service has the "breathing room" to rank and bring you ready-to-buy customers [9, 10].

### 2) The Exact Steps or Prompt to Run This Week

To structure your new WordPress website (`pineappleroofingllc.com`) correctly:
1.  Create a single **Parent Hub page** for your services (e.g., `yourdomain.com/frisco-roofing-services/`) [11, 12].
2.  Create **Child pages** for each specific service (e.g., `yourdomain.com/frisco-roofing-services/storm-damage-repair/`) [11, 13].
3.  In WordPress, go to your **Pages** dashboard, click **Quick Edit** under your child pages, and set the **Parent** dropdown to your main `/frisco-roofing-services/` page [14]. This nested folder structure keeps your links clean and logical for AI crawlers [14, 15].
4.  Repeat the same parent-child structure for your local area served pages [13].
