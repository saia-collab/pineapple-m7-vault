## 2. GEO, SEO & DIGITAL LEAD ENGINES

### Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) Framework
Traditional Search Engine Optimization (SEO) designed for blue links is obsolete. PM7 optimizes for **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)**—structuring our site's content specifically to be crawled, indexed, and cited by Generative AI search engines (such as ChatGPT Search, Perplexity Pro, and Google AI Overviews). 

According to peer-reviewed Princeton and industry research, **72% of all AI-cited content** uses highly structured, easily extractable paragraph text. We achieve this through the **Content Capsule Technique**:

*   **Structural Rule**: Place a high-intent local query inside an **H2 or H3 question header**.
*   **The Capsule Rule**: Directly below the question header, write an answer paragraph exactly **30 to 60 words (under 300 characters)** in length.
*   **The Isolation Rule**: The Content Capsule must be a standalone answer that can be understood without reading any other part of the page. It must contain **zero internal or outbound links** inside the capsule text itself to ensure clean, uninterrupted extraction by LLM scrapers.
*   **The Credential Rule**: Every Content Capsule must naturally weave in at least one primary credential (`RCAT #03-0637`, `IKO Certified`, or the `Complimentary Professional Photo Audit (CPPA)`).
