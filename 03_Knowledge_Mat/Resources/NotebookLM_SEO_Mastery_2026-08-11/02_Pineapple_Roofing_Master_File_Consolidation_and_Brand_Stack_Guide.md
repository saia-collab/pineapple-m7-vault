### 1. Plain-English "What It Is + Why It Matters for a Frisco Roofer"

**Master File Consolidation** is the process of merging multiple small checklists, spreadsheets, SOPs, and text notes into a single, structured "Master Document" before uploading it [1-4]. 

*   **Jargon Glossary:**
    *   **Context Stack**: A unified, single-source-of-truth document containing all your company's localized facts, unique experiences, and brand rules so the AI never has to guess [5, 6].
    *   **Source Limit**: The maximum number of individual files (exactly 300) that you can upload to a single notebook project [7].

#### Why It Matters to Pineapple Roofing:
If you try to upload every neighborhood targeting checklist, daily task list, storm damage blog outline, and local GBP review draft as separate files, you will hit the **300-source limit** instantly [7]. 

By merging all these fragmented documents into a single **"Pineapple Roofing Brand Bible,"** you free up dozens of slots in your notebook. The AI actually performs much better because all your critical business constraints—like your **RCAT #03-0637** license, **IKO Certified** standards, and your high-value **Complimentary Professional Property Assessment (CPPA)** framework—live in the exact same document, preventing the AI from getting confused across disconnected files [1, 2, 8, 9].
