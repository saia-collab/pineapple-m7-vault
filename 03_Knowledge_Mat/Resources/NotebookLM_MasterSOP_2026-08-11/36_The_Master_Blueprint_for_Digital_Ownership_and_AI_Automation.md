### **Step 3: Paste the Config into Your AI Client** [cite: 240, 275]

## 🏗️ 4. THE NON-TECHNICAL SCORPION DETACHMENT & CLONE WORKFLOW

Because you own your domain name (`pineapplecontractors.com`), **you do not need to let the marketing agency hold your digital presence hostage.** [cite: 134] The single most important rule is: **Build and complete your WordPress site (`pineappleroofingllc.com`) first, and repoint GoDaddy DNS last.** [cite: 136]

### **Step A: The Zero-Code Feature & Review Extraction**
You do not have to write HTML or figure out Elementor coordinates [cite: 120, 138]. Your pre-built workspace already has **33 fully responsive, Brand Law-compliant Elementor Canvas blocks** staged and ready for you (located under your `02_Workspaces/2026-07-23_SEO_Site_Migration/` directory) [cite: 166, 167]:

3. Click **Edit with Elementor**, drag in a single **HTML widget** to the blank canvas, and paste the pre-staged block code matching that page (Frisco, Allen, Gutter Repair, etc.) [cite: 138, 167]. 
4. Ensure the URL slug exactly matches your old page (e.g., `locations/allen-tx`) and hit **Publish** [cite: 138].

### **Step B: Safe Redirects (Saving Your SEO Clicks)** [cite: 115]
Scorpion structured your site with long, bloated URLs (like `/service-areas/collin-county-tx/allen/`) [cite: 141]. Your clean WordPress site uses modern paths (`/locations/allen-tx/`) [cite: 141]. 1. On your new WordPress site, install and activate the free plugin **"Redirection"** [cite: 115, 141].
2. Go to **Tools \\(\rightarrow\\) Redirection \\(\rightarrow\\) Import** [cite: 115, 141].
3. Upload the pre-mapped **`M7_Redirect_Map.csv`** file from your workspace [cite: 115, 141, 167].
4. This instantly establishes permanent **301 redirects** [cite: 115, 194]. Every historical click from your old domain will now automatically route to the corresponding page on your new WordPress site [cite: 115, 194].

### **Step C: The Domain Takeover (Repointing GoDaddy)** [cite: 136, 140]
Once your 33 pages are live on WordPress, verified clean, and your 301 redirects are imported, execute the takeover [cite: 136, 140, 169]:
1. Go to your GoDaddy account, select your domain (`pineapplecontractors.com`), and navigate to **DNS Settings** [cite: 136, 140].
2. Update your **Nameservers** or your **A-Record IP** to point to your new WordPress hosting server [cite: 136, 140].
3. Once DNS propagates (1–24 hours), your domain will now serve your fast, owned WordPress site [cite: 140, 141]. Scorpion's locked site will disappear from the public web [cite: 141].
4. **Change your GBP website link** from the old layout to point to your fresh site to keep reviews flowing to your new flagship [cite: 117, 181]!
