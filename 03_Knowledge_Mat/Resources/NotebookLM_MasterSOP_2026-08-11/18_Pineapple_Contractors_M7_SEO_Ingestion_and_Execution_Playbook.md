### 📈 1. RUNNING THE GOOGLE SEARCH CONSOLE (GSC) STRIKING DISTANCE SCAN

In search engine optimization, **"Striking Distance" keywords (positions 5–20)** represent your single lowest-hanging fruit [cite: 176, 336]. Google already recognizes your authority for these terms and displays your site to searchers, but you are not getting clicks because you aren't in the top 3 spots [cite: 229, 336]. Moving these up is the fastest way to drive inbound phone calls [cite: 336].

To run this scan and isolate your target keywords, execute the **M7 SEO Ingestion Pipeline** [cite: 57, 143]:

```

│  GSC Performance  │      │  Engine (Custom Gem)     │      │  active_context/ │
└───────────────────┘      └──────────────────────────┘      └──────────────────┘

#### Step A: Export the Raw Data from GSC
1. Log into your Google Search Console dashboard (`search.google.com/search-console`) [cite: 336].
2. Select your verified domain property (**`pineapplecontractors.com`** or **`pineappleroofingllc.com`**) [cite: 172, 336].
3. Navigate to **Search Results / Performance**, set your date filter to the **last 90 days** (or 28 days for rapid storm tracking) [cite: 33, 338], and click **Export \\(\rightarrow\\) Download CSV** [cite: 67, 338]. Open the **"Queries"** sheet [cite: 67].

#### Step B: Ingest & Filter via the GSC Analytics Gem
To prevent manual spreadsheet formatting and filter the raw noise, feed the CSV data block directly into your **PM7 GSC Analytics Engine** custom Gem [cite: 63, 67]. The Gem runs on this exact mathematical sorting logic [cite: 65]:
*   **The Filter:** It automatically isolates all search queries where the **average position is between 10.0 and 25.0** and **Impressions are greater than 100** [cite: 65]. 
*   **The Intent Sort:** It categorizes these keywords into **Transactional** (containing terms like *repair, installation, company, near me*) and **Informational** (such as *leaking, storm damage, claim*) [cite: 65].

#### Step C: Save to Your Shared Memory Vault
Copy the structured markdown table generated by your custom Gem and drop it into your local directory as a new file [cite: 67]:  
📁 `C:\Pineapple Contractors M7\03_Knowledge_Mat\active_context\gsc_striking_distance.md` [cite: 1, 67, 264]
