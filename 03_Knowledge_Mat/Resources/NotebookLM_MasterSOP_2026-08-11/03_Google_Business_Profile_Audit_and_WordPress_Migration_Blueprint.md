### 📍 PART 1: HOW TO RUN A GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE AUDIT

Your Google Business Profile is your most valuable piece of digital real estate, driving the vast majority of your inbound phone calls [cite: 136, 334]. To run a comprehensive audit of your listing against your top three local competitors, execute these four audit passes in **Claude Cowork** or your **local Agent OS** [cite: 127, 330, 332]:

#### **Pass 1: The Category Audit** [cite: 334]
1.  **Search your target keywords** (such as *"roof replacement Frisco TX"*, *"Frisco storm damage restoration"*) inside Google Maps [cite: 335].
2.  Identify the top 3 ranking competitors for each search term [cite: 335].
3.  Extract their **primary and secondary categories** [cite: 335]. *(For example: if a competitor is using "Water Damage Restoration Service" as a secondary category and you are missing it, you won't show up for emergency late-night storm damage searches) [cite: 334].*
4.  Compile these into a spreadsheet and highlight the missing categories in red [cite: 335, 336]. Add these categories directly to your active profiles [cite: 336].

#### **Pass 2: The Attributes Audit** [cite: 337]
1.  Verify that your profile attributes are fully configured [cite: 337]. 
2.  Google uses attributes to match your business to highly specific search queries [cite: 337]. Ensure that vital local service trust attributes—specifically **"Open 24/7"**—are checked [cite: 337]. This signals to Google’s algorithm to display your map listing when a homeowner searches for a 24-hour contractor after an evening storm [cite: 337].

#### **Pass 3: The Services Section Audit** [cite: 343]
1.  Compare your GBP services list against your competitor listings [cite: 344].
2.  For every service you offer (e.g., roof replacement, gutter installation, storm damage repair), write a highly optimized, unique description of **2–3 sentences (40–60 words)** [cite: 344, 345]. 
3.  Each description must naturally mention your target service keyword, reference key neighborhoods in your target territory (like *Starwood* or *Newman Village*), and describe a specific customer benefit [cite: 345]. This provides free keyword placement that Google's crawlers index to match you with local searches [cite: 340, 343].

#### **Pass 4: The Photo Count & Freshness Audit** [cite: 348]
1.  Review your competitors' photo sections to estimate their photo upload counts over the last 30, 60, and 90 days [cite: 349].
2.  Google rewards fresh, active profiles with higher Map Pack prominence [cite: 341, 348]. 
3.  Audit your photo library and schedule a recurring loop to upload **3–5 high-quality job site or team photos every week** [cite: 348]. Pull these from your **39GB media drive** and geotag them to your primary DFW neighborhoods before uploading [cite: 189, 222].
