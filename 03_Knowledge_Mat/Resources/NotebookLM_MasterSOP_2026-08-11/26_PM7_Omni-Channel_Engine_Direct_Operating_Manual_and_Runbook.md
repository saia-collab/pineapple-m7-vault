``` [cite: 96, 103, 104]
## 🌐 3. EXPLAINED: THE 5-SITE SEO FLYWHEEL (EVERYWHERE ENGINE)

The **Five-Site Flywheel** (or Everywhere Engine) is an advanced organic SEO architecture built specifically to address the modern reality of search engine optimization: **68% of Google searches now end in zero clicks** because Google reads the answers directly to users in AI Overviews [cite: 44, 468, 471].

```
               [1 Keyword + 1 Case Study] 
                           │
                           ▼
                 [Hermes SEO Profile] 
                           │
          ┌────────────────┼────────────────┐
          ▼                ▼                ▼
     [Static Site 1]  [Static Site 2]  [Static Site 3]... (up to 5)
          │                │                │
          └────────────────┼────────────────┘
                           ▼
                    [The Mention Web]
              (AI associates your brand name 
               with target search queries)
``` [cite: 318, 320, 322]

### How the Flywheel Operates:
1.  **Single Ingestion Input:** You provide just **one high-performing keyword** and **one real-world local case study** or video transcript (your source of truth) [cite: 23, 301, 322].
2.  **Unique Multi-Site Output:** The AI writer automatically crafts **five completely unique articles**—each featuring a different opening hook, varied SEO titles, distinct structural formatting, and custom FAQs [cite: 24, 25, 301, 322].
3.  **Lightweight Static Builds:** The pages are compiled locally in your workspace via Eleventy or Astro and deployed instantly to Netlify in parallel using the Netlify CLI [cite: 2, 26, 322].
4.  **Instant same-day Indexing:** The system triggers an API call to **Indexceptional** or **Omega Indexer**, getting your live pages crawled and indexed by Google within hours [cite: 27, 28, 34, 421].
5.  **The Cross-Linking Moat (Pass the Juice):** Every post automatically embeds an **"Also On Our Network"** cross-linking block [cite: 29]. By linking the five small regional sites together, they pass search authority to one another and train crawling spiders to quickly index the smaller domains [cite: 29].
6.  **The Mention Web:** When AI search engines (like Perplexity, ChatGPT, or Gemini) crawl the web, they notice multiple independent domains and platforms constantly mentioning your brand name (*Pineapple Roofing*) next to local service keywords [cite: 41, 416, 433]. This trains their neural networks to recommend you as the trusted local authority, driving up your organic click-through rates [cite: 41, 416, 433].
```
