### 🏛️ THE "KARPATHY LLM WIKI" ARCHITECTURE

*   👉 **`inbox/`** — Where incoming, unverified notes, client session transcriptions, and raw campaign ideas are dropped [cite: 150, 260].
*   👉 **`raw/`** — Where untidy, raw text summaries, PDF scans, and daily developer logs are stored [cite: 8, 260].
*   👉 **`sources/`** — The verified truth substrate. Once notes are audited and cleaned by your **Brand Firewall**, they are promoted to permanent nodes [cite: 26, 260].
