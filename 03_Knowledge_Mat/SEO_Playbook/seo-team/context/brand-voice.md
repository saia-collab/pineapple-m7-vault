# Brand Voice — Pineapple Roofing (PM7)

## Tone in three words

Authoritative, family-proud, zero-fluff. (Polynesian-owned, hardworking, honest.)

## The reader

- **Who's reading:** DFW homeowners right after a hail/storm — worried about hidden damage, insurance, and being taken advantage of. Also commercial property managers.
- **Reading level:** plain English; a few roofing terms OK if explained.
- **What they're afraid of:** getting ripped off, a fly-by-night "storm chaser," a botched claim.

## Say / Don't say

| Always | Never |
|--------|-------|
| Complimentary Professional Photo Audit (CPPA) | "free", "free inspection", "free quote" |
| IKO Certified | "GAF" |
| The Pineapple Standard | "warrior", "toa", "six brothers" |
| Full Restoration Coverage | "$0 down", "$0 out of pocket" |
| RCAT #03-0637, since 2005, (972) 928-0788 | vague "licensed & insured" with no number |
| Royal Navy #1A365D / Gold #FBC02D / Cyan #00BFFF | the color green (banned everywhere) |
| family-owned, documented, honest | "cheap", "bargain", "discount", hype, exclamation-spam |

## Sentence style

- **Person:** first person — "we document every hit," "our crew cleans up daily."
- **Contractions:** yes ("we'll," "you're") — reads human.
- **Humour:** none-to-dry. This is trust + money, not jokes.
- **Structure (AEO):** answer the searcher's exact question in sentence one, then prove it.

## Words from the client's own mouth

> "We're not a crew that drives in after a storm — we have a verified office right here."
> "Most hail damage is invisible from the ground. We document every hit with photos your insurance can actually use."
> "The name on the truck is the name on the license."
