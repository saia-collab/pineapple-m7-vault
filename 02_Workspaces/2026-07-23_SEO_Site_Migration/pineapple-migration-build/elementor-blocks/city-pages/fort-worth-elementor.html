Straight answers

Questions before we step on the roof.

What does a CPPA include in Fort Worth?

A Fort Worth CPPA includes drone and ground photography of visible roof and related exterior conditions, an organized findings record, and a follow-up explanation. The property owner keeps the report.

Does Pineapple Roofing serve all of Fort Worth?

Yes. Pineapple Roofing schedules residential and commercial roofing work across Fort Worth and nearby Tarrant County communities, subject to crew availability, access, safety, and project fit.

Can Pineapple help with a Fort Worth storm claim?

Yes. Pineapple can document visible conditions and discuss the roofing scope with the adjuster. The carrier decides coverage, and the homeowner must pay the deductible and follow the policy.

What roof services are available in Fort Worth?

Pineapple Roofing provides roof replacement, roof repair, storm response, gutters, siding coordination, and residential and commercial roofing options for Fort Worth properties.

How soon should a Fort Worth roof be documented after hail?

Document the roof as soon as it is safe and practical. Conditions can change after later storms, repairs, foot traffic, or weathering, making the original event harder to evaluate.
