## PERFORMANCE METRICS & KILL/SCALE RULES

### What to Measure
- **Click-Through Rate (CTR):** Target 1.5%+ (kill below 1% after 1,000 impressions)
- **Form Submission Rate:** Target 15–25% of clicks → form submission
- **Cost Per Lead (CPL):** Target under $50 (max $75 if lead quality is high)
- **Phone Call Rate:** Target 30–40% of leads call within 24 hours (measure via call tracking)
- **Appointment Booking Rate:** Target 40%+ of leads → booked audit

### Kill Rule
If CTR drops below 1% or CPL exceeds $100 after 1,000 impressions, pause that ad variation immediately and redeploy budget to a winning ad.

### Scale Rule
If CTR exceeds 2.0% and CPL is under $40, increase budget by 20% and continue monitoring.

### Frequency Monitoring
If frequency (average times a user sees the ad) exceeds 2.5, rotate to a new ad variation to prevent ad fatigue.
