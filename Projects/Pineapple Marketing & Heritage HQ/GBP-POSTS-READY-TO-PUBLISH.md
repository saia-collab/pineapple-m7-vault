## PHOTO SOURCING

**Where to Get Images:**
1. **Your own jobs** (best): Before/after photos from recent projects
2. **Stock photos** (if necessary): Unsplash, Pexels (search "roof," "hail damage," "storm")
3. **Team photos:** Your crew at job sites (blurred faces if needed)
4. **Equipment photos:** Equipter, thermal camera, drones

**Photo Quality Standards:**
- Minimum 1200px wide (Google compresses but needs high-res input)
- Good lighting (avoid blurry, dark, or backlit)
- Landscape orientation preferred (wider)
- Include people when possible (adds human touch)

## POSTING FREQUENCY

**Minimum:** 1 post per week (what we recommend above)

**Aggressive:** 2 posts per week (double posts on Mon + Thu)
