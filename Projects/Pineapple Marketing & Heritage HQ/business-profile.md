- **What:** Family-operated by 6 brothers with Tongan cultural values of integrity
- **Why:** In a world of faceless corporate franchises, your "The Pineapple Standard-level" commitment to craftsmanship is genuinely different

- **What:** You handle roof + gutters + fencing + painting + flooring in one contract
