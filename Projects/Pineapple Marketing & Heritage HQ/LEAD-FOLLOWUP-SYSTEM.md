## SYSTEM OVERVIEW

**Core Principle:** Speed wins. First responder takes 78% of leads.

**Your Competitive Edge:** 5-minute response time (while competitors sleep on leads for 24 hours).

**What This Does:**
- Auto-responds to every incoming lead within 60 seconds (via SMS + email)
- Phones you immediately so YOU can call back within 5 minutes
- Schedules appointment automatically (reduces back-and-forth)
- Follows up systematically if lead doesn't respond (but stops the moment they do)

#### HOUR 1: LIVE PHONE CALL (YOU CALL THEM)

**Phone System Alert:** Your phone buzzes. Jobnimbus automatically flags: "[Name] - Lead from website form. Waiting for call."

**Your 5-Minute Script:**

```
YOU: "Hey [Name], this is Saia from Pineapple Roofing. I got your request for a Complimentary photo audit. You still interested?"

THEM: [Gives number]
```

**Call Duration:** 2–4 minutes  
**Why It Works:** 
- You're confirming urgency + interest (filtering out "just curious" leads)
- You're setting specific expectations (reduces no-shows)
- You're removing pressure ("no sales pitch")
- You're building familiarity ("I'll send you the report")
- You're staying in touch ("we'll text 20 min out")

## QUALIFICATION FRAMEWORK

**High-Intent Leads (Act Fast):**
- Recent hail/storm damage
- Visible leak or water damage
- Existing roof 15+ years old
- Planning to sell soon
- Just got insurance claim approved

**Medium-Intent Leads (Move at Normal Pace):**
- Routine inspection
- "Just getting quotes"
- Wants to compare prices
- Not urgent timeline

**Low-Intent Leads (Polite Exit):**
- Renters (you can't help them)
- Apartment buildings (out of scope)
- Just curious, no roof problem
- Unresponsive after 3 attempts

**Script to Disqualify:** "Hey [Name], thanks for your interest. Quick question—are you the homeowner? Or renting?" 

If renting: "Got it. Unfortunately, we work with homeowners directly. You'd need to have your landlord reach out. Good luck though!"

## CRITICAL RULES (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

### Speed
- Minute 0: Auto SMS + Auto Email
- Hour 1: Live phone call (YOU call them)
- Day 1: Follow-up text if no response
- Never leave a lead sitting for more than 5 hours

### Frequency
- Max 1 text per day
- Max 1 email every 2 days
- Never before 8am or after 8pm
- Stop immediately when they respond

### Stopping Rule
- The MOMENT a lead says "Not interested" or "Stop contacting me" — STOP. Don't send another message.
- If they go silent for 7 days after initial contact, they're gone. Archive them. Retarget via ads.

### Tone
- You by name (not "Pineapple Roofing team")
- Conversational (not corporate)
- Direct (no fluff)
- Helpful (solve their problem, don't sell them)
