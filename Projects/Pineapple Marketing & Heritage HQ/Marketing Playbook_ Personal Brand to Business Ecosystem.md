# Marketing Playbook: Personal Brand to Business Ecosystem

This playbook outlines a strategic framework to leverage the personal brand of **Tatafu Nunumoveisini Moeakiola Veehala** to drive growth, reach, and conversion for **Pineapple Roofing & Contractors**. By bridging the gap between high-performance entrepreneurial content and professional roofing services, we will create a "Trust-First" marketing engine.

## 1. Core Marketing Strategy: The "Bridge" Framework

The current data shows high engagement with motivational and success-oriented content. We will bridge this to the roofing business using three distinct content pillars:

| Pillar | Focus | Goal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Mindset** | Success, discipline, and personal growth. | Build authority and reach. |
| **The Craft** | Behind-the-scenes of roofing, quality control, and "made sweeter" service. | Demonstrate expertise and reliability. |
| **The Result** | Before/After, homeowner testimonials, and insurance claim wins. | Drive direct leads and conversions. |

## 2. Meta Ads Optimization Plan

Based on the account audit, we will shift from simple post boosting to a structured lead-generation funnel.

### Audience Targeting Strategy
- **Primary Audience**: Homeowners in the DFW Metroplex (Dallas, Fort Worth, Lewisville, Frisco, McKinney).
- **Demographics**: Men & Women, Ages 30–65 (Shift from current 25–44 male skew to include decision-making homeowners).
- **Interests**: Home improvement, real estate investing, renovation, and local DFW community groups.

### Platform Shift
We will increase allocation to **Instagram Feed and Reels**, leveraging the existing @saiamoe visual content which is currently under-utilized in the ad account (only ~2% of impressions).

## 4. Implementation Checklist (The 7-Day Plan)

1.  **Day 1: Pixel Setup**: Ensure the Meta Pixel is firing "Lead" events on the `pineapplecontractors.com` confirmation page.
2.  **Day 2: Audience Expansion**: Create a Lookalike Audience (1%) based on existing high-engaging Accounts Center accounts.
3.  **Day 3: Lead Form Creation**: Build a native Meta Lead Form that captures Name, Phone, Email, and "Type of Service Needed."
4.  **Day 4: Launch "The Craft" Campaign**: Use the mansion-knocking Reel to drive brand awareness in high-income ZIP codes.
5.  **Day 5: Launch "The Result" Campaign**: Target storm-affected areas with the "Insurance Claim Expert" angle.
6.  **Day 6: Retargeting Setup**: Create a retargeting ad for anyone who engaged with personal posts but hasn't requested a quote.
7.  **Day 7: Performance Review**: Audit initial CTR (link click-through rate) and CPC (cost per link click).

## 5. Kill Rules & KPI Benchmarks

| Metric | Benchmark | Action if Below |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CTR (link click-through rate)** | > 1.5% | Swap creative asset or headline. |
| **Cost per result (Lead)** | < $35.00 | Audit landing page or lead form friction. |
| **Frequency** | < 4.0 | Refresh creative or expand audience size. |

> **Marketing Director's Note**: Success in the DFW roofing market is built on speed and trust. By using your personal brand as the face of Pineapple Roofing, we eliminate the "faceless contractor" problem and build immediate rapport with homeowners.
