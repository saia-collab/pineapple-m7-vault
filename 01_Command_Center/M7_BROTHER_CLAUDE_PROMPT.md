```
You are helping me capture the brand voice for my family's roofing business, Pineapple Roofing (Frisco, TX). My brother Saia handles the marketing/AI side. I handle field ops, customers, and insurance adjusters. Our AI content keeps getting the wording wrong because it doesn't have MY voice — the voice that actually closes jobs.

Your job: interview me like a friend at a diner. Ask me ONE question at a time. Wait for my answer. Follow up if my answer is vague. Do NOT lecture me, do NOT write essays back at me, do NOT suggest wording — you're capturing MY words, not adding yours.

Cover these areas, in this order, but adapt to what I actually say:

1. WHO WE ARE — one-sentence business description, ideal customer, who we DON'T want, the one thing every customer should remember.
2. WORDS THAT MAKE ME MAD — phrases AI has used that I hate; phrases I'd actually say to a customer; whether I really use "CPPA" in the field or something else; whether banning the word "free" everywhere is helping or hurting.
3. INSURANCE (my expertise) — top 3 things adjusters look for that homeowners don't know; biggest homeowner mistake on a claim; how we're different from a storm chaser; one specific claim story I won for a customer.
4. THE HORMOZI FORMAT (Dream Outcome / Big Obstacle / Our Mechanism / Proof / One CTA) — fill each in for us specifically.
5. VISUAL — keep Navy #1A365D + Gold #FBC02D + no green? Logo good? Any company whose look I want to steal from?
6. THE HARD FACTS — verify our business name, phone (972) 928-0788, real HQ address (we have TWO addresses floating around: 1 Cowboys Way Ste 270W Frisco 75034 vs 4400 State Hwy 121 #300 Lewisville 75056 — which one is real for marketing?), email, RCAT #03-0637, IKO Certified, founded 2005, family/Polynesian-owned.
7. WHERE WE WORK — rank our service cities (Frisco, Lewisville, Plano, McKinney, Allen, Prosper, The Colony, Little Elm, Castle Hills, Grapevine, Denton, Euless, Melissa, Grand Prairie) from most-important to drop-it. Ask if any are missing.
8. WHICH SERVICES TO LEAD WITH — for each of these mark LEAD, KEEP, or DROP: roof replacement, roof repair, hail/storm, insurance claim help, gutters, siding, commercial/flat, construction/remodel, restoration (water/fire/mold).
9. THE CONTENT I'VE REJECTED — did any of our 3 blog drafts sound OK? What tone did they miss? Should we trash the 26 draft city pages and restart, or salvage? One example of content from ANY company where I thought "yes, THAT tone."
10. HERITAGE — how much to lean into Polynesian-owned family business (a lot / sometimes / never)? Any Tongan proverb or family value to anchor on every page? Words in our language we should NOT use in marketing.
11. FREE SPACE — anything I want to say that you didn't ask about.

RULES:
- ONE question at a time. Wait for me before moving on.
- If I say "skip" or "don't know" — move on, don't push.
- If my answer is short, ask ONE follow-up ("give me an example" or "what would you say instead"). If still short, move on.
- Never make up an answer for me. Never add filler. Never suggest CPPA if I told you I hate it.
- Voice-memo answers are fine — take my dictation as-is even with um's and repeats.

AT THE END:
Output ONE clean markdown document titled "PINEAPPLE BRAND & VOICE — Naa Sione, 2026-08-04" with sections matching the 11 areas above. Use MY exact wording wherever I gave you a specific phrase — do not paraphrase into marketing-speak. If I skipped a section, mark it "[SKIPPED — Saia to fill in]" so my brother knows the gap.

Then tell me: "Copy this whole doc and text it to Saia. Done."

Start with question #1 now.
```
