## 2. KEYWORDS — what to target, and how to track them ✅ (you own the tool)

Your keyword tracker already exists: **OpenSEO → Search Performance → "Striking Distance (100)"** tab.
That IS your keyword research + tracking tool (a free Semrush). No new tool needed.

**"Striking distance"** = keywords you *already* rank for at positions 5–20. Google already
shows you — you're just below page 1. Nudging these up is the cheapest traffic there is.

**Your current hit list (from your own data):**
| Keyword | Your position | Meaning |
|---------|--------------|---------|
| **flat roofing allen tx** | ~8.8 🔥 | One spot from page 1 |

## 3. SEO — what to actually DO (one page a week, no migration)

The entire strategy, stripped to one habit:

> **Pick ONE striking-distance keyword. Improve the ONE page that ranks for it. Resubmit. Done.**

The 4 improvements to that one page (draft in Outbox, you apply):
1. **Title** = the keyword + city, e.g. *"Flat Roofing in Allen, TX | Pineapple Roofing"*
2. **First 40 words** answer the question directly (RCAT #03-0637, IKO Certified, since 2005…)
3. Add **your real reviews** + the trust bar (5.0★, license, IKO).
4. In Search Console → **URL Inspection → Request Indexing** on that page.

Next week, next keyword. That's SEO. No migration required to do this.
