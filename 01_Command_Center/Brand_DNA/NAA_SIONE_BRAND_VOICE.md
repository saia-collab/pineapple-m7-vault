# 🍍 PINEAPPLE BRAND & VOICE — Naa Sione

## 1. Who We Are
**What we do (one sentence):** Residential and commercial roofing and general contracting — roof repairs, replacements, and emergency repairs across all systems (shingles, tile, stone-coated steel, metal standing seam, metal roofs, custom metal coping, gutters and downspouts, and more).

**Ideal customer:** Homeowner in any neighborhood, property value $300,000+, roof older than 4-5 years, hail or windstorm in the past year. Also commercial via general contractors, builders, realtors, property managers.

**Who we don't want:** Price shoppers — anyone chasing the cheapest bid over doing it right.

**The ONE thing customers remember:** The sweetest experience — transparency, high-quality materials, workmanship, and service. Model: **"Roofing Made Sweeter."**

## 2. Words — Banned / Natural / "Free" / CPPA
**Hard legal rule (priority over style):** nothing implying free roof work, waived deductibles, or fee inducement tied to insurance claims (Texas Dept. of Insurance / consumer-protection risk).

**Natural field voice:** "We work with the insurance carriers directly. Our team has over 20 years of experience… we understand the playbook dealing with carriers and getting full approval to get your property restored" — roof, gutters, downspouts, window screens, drywall, paint/patch, garage door, chimney cap, AC, etc. "We are not only a roofing contractor, we are a general contractor who partners with highly vetted subcontractors to get the full scope done that the carrier typically pays for."

**CPPA:** NOT used in the field. Field term = **"storm damage report"** or **"licensed inspection roof report."** No "free" attached.

**"Free" ban — MODIFIED:** "Free" is fine for **inspections/estimates** ("free roof inspection/estimate" as a CTA). Stays banned anywhere it implies free repair work, waived costs, or anything tied to an insurance claim payout.

## 3. Insurance & Claims
**Top 3 adjusters look for:** (1) Collateral/ground damage — gutters, downspouts, window screens, fence, garage door, exterior lights. (2) Hail bruises across all 4 slopes — need 6-10 per slope for full replacement; 3 of 4 slopes damaged typically warrants full replacement. (3) Interior leaks/water damage — homeowner can often file a separate personal-property claim.

**Biggest homeowner mistake:** Wrong date of loss (too-old storm date lets the carrier deny). Also going to their insurance agent first (biased against filing) instead of a licensed roof specialist.

**Vs a storm chaser:** Local, permanent presence in Frisco. 20+ yrs combined experience. Real reviews + track record (residential + commercial). On-site 24/7 foreman. Vetted crews. Carries general liability, auto, pollution, umbrella. Bondable. Family-owned.

**Claim story (proof):** Fort Worth homeowner, ~10-yr roof + solar, USAA. Adjuster first denied / partial (~$2K, under the ~$8-9K deductible = effectively $0). Pineapple submitted a supplement with photo evidence, pushed re-inspection; new adjuster approved full replacement — roof, solar system, gutters, fence, interior. **~$3,000 → ~$50,000 full approval.**
