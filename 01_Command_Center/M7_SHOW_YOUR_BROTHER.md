# 🍍 What We Built — In Plain English

## The one-sentence version
**We turned 39GB of job footage sitting in Google Drive into a machine that posts branded, on-message content to every platform — without hiring an agency.**

---

## 🔁 THE PIPELINE (this is the whole thing)

```
  📁 39GB Drive          ✂️ Video Editor        ✍️ Caption Pack        📅 Blotato          👍 Saia
  (raw job footage)  →   (trim + captions)  →   (already written)  →  (schedules all)  →  (approves)
```

**Five steps. That's it.** Here's each one:

### 1️⃣ MEDIA — the Drive is organized so nothing is lost
```
PINEAPPLE_MEDIA_HUB/
├── 00_INBOX/            ← crew drops phone footage here
├── 01_READY_TO_POST/    ← trimmed + ready (this is the only folder we post from)
├── 02_BY_SERVICE/       ← roofing / storm / commercial
├── 03_BY_CITY/          ← Frisco / Lewisville / McKinney
├── 04_TEAM_BRAND/       ← crew, family, culture
└── 04_FINAL_FOR_BLATATO/← captioned, scheduled-ready
```
**Rule:** we only post from `01_READY_TO_POST`. If it's not trimmed, it doesn't go out.

### 2️⃣ CUT IT — the Video Editor does the editing
Open **Local Studio → Video Editor**, drop in a clip, and type:
> *"Cut to the strongest 20 seconds. Remove dead air and umms. Burn bold 2-word captions."*

No editor to hire. No Premiere. **One sentence, one video.**

### 3️⃣ CAPTION — already written, brand-safe
Every caption is **pre-written and checked** so nobody ever posts the wrong thing:
- ❌ Never "free" → ✅ **Complimentary Professional Photo Audit (CPPA)**
- ❌ Never "GAF" → ✅ **IKO Certified**
- ✅ Always: **RCAT #03-0637 · (972) 928-0788**

Files ready to use: `Social_Hormozi_CaptionPack.md` · `Reel_Repurpose_Plan.md` · `Social_CaptionPack_Week_AllPlatforms.md`

### 4️⃣ SCHEDULE — Blotato posts everywhere at once
One upload → Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, X, YouTube. **Scheduled, not published.**

### 5️⃣ APPROVE — nothing goes live without Saia
Everything sits **PAUSED** until Saia says GO. **That's the safety net** — no robot ever posts on its own.
