# 📈 M7 Marketing SOP (Framework-Aligned)

**How to use:** every marketing task starts by naming the **skill category** and the
**framework rule** applied. All outputs stay in M7 brand law (CPPA, IKO, Navy/Gold, zero green).

---

## 1. FOUNDATION — Product-Marketing Context (Source of Truth)
*Framework: `product-marketing`. Do this FIRST; everything references it.*

Maintain one living file: `03_Knowledge_Mat/active_context/product_marketing.md` with:
- **Audience:** DFW homeowners (35–65) + commercial/property managers with storm/aging roofs.
- **Positioning:** The local, family-run, IKO Certified team that documents damage and handles the insurance claim *with* you. "The Pineapple Standard."
- **Value prop:** Complimentary Professional Photo Audit → insurance-ready report → done-right roof.
- **Proof:** since 2005 · RCAT #03-0637 · IKO Certified · 5-star (400+) · Polynesian-owned.
- **Offer:** CPPA (micro-win) → inspection/proposal (macro-sale).

**Rule:** never write copy or SEO without pulling from this file first. Update it whenever
positioning, services, or offers change.

---

## 2. SEO & DISCOVERY WORKFLOW
*Frameworks: `seo-audit`, `ai-seo`, `competitors`.*

**A. SEO Audit (`seo-audit`) — monthly**
1. Pull GSC data (OpenSEO → Search Performance).
2. Isolate **striking-distance** keywords (position 10–25, impressions >100).
3. Tag intent: Transactional (cost, quote, contractor, near me) vs Informational (how to, signs of, storm damage).
4. For each: decide **optimize existing page** or **build new**.

**B. AI Search Optimization (`ai-seo`)**
- Put the **direct answer in the first sentence** of every page/post (Google AI Mode + AI Overviews pull these).
- Add a clear **FAQ** section (5–8 Q&As) to each money page.
- Use **LocalBusiness JSON-LD schema** (in the Goldie Week 1–2 pack).

**C. Competitor Comparison Pages (`competitors`)**
- Build high-intent pages: "Pineapple vs [competitor]" and "[City] roofing companies compared."
- Structure: honest comparison table → why us (IKO, RCAT, insurance help) → CPPA CTA.

---

## 3. SITE ARCHITECTURE + CRO + COPY
*Frameworks: `site-architecture`, `cro`, `copywriting`.*

**Site architecture (`site-architecture`)** — hub-and-spoke:
- **Hubs:** Roofing Services · Restoration · Construction · Service Areas.
- **Spokes:** each service + each city page links back to its hub. Internal-link every page.

**Copywriting (`copywriting`)** — every page:
- First sentence = the direct answer / dream outcome.
- 5th-grade reading level, value-per-second, no fluff.
- One clear CTA repeated: **Book Your CPPA / (972) 928-0788**.

**CRO (`cro`)** — continuous:
- Every page: sticky click-to-call, lead form above the fold, trust bar (IKO/RCAT/5-star).
- Test one thing at a time (headline, CTA text, form length). Keep what wins.
- 5-minute lead response = the biggest conversion lever you have.

## 4. MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE (keep the playbook alive)

**Weekly**

**Monthly**

- [ ] Build 1 competitor/comparison page + 1 city page
- [ ] Review GBP Insights + LSA cost-per-lead; dispute junk LSA leads
