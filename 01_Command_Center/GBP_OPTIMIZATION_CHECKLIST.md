# 📍 GBP Optimization Checklist — Make the Free Leads Multiply

> You're already getting LSA + local leads with no effort. This is the one-time
> tune-up that makes Google show you MORE, higher, more often. Do it once at
> business.google.com, then just post weekly. ~45 minutes total.

## 1. Core info (get every field exact + consistent)
- [ ] **Business name** — exactly "Pineapple Contractors" (no keyword stuffing).
- [ ] **Primary category** — "Roofing Contractor". Add secondary: "Construction Company", "Water Damage Restoration Service", "Commercial Roofing".
- [ ] **Phone** — (972) 928-0788 (same everywhere — site, GBP, LSA).
- [ ] **Website** — your main site URL.
- [ ] **Hours** — accurate, including "Open 24 hours" for emergency if true.
- [ ] **Service area** — list every city: Frisco, Plano, McKinney, Grapevine, Allen, Denton, Lewisville, + Collin & Tarrant counties.

## 2. Services (add ALL of them — each is a keyword)
Add every service with a short description + "CPPA" language:
- [ ] Roof Repair · Roof Replacement · Storm/Hail Damage · Leak Repair
- [ ] Residential Roofing · Commercial Roofing (TPO/PVC/Metal) · Metal Roofing
- [ ] Gutter Installation · Siding · Insurance Claim Assistance
- [ ] Water/Fire/Mold/Storm Restoration
- [ ] Construction & Remodeling

## 3. Photos (this drives ranking + calls — you have 39GB!)
- [ ] Logo + cover photo
- [ ] **15–20 real job photos** (before/after, drone, finished roofs, crew) — Google favors profiles with fresh photos
- [ ] Add a few **every week** (from your media folders)
- [ ] Add the **"pineapple website video.MOV"** as a video

## 4. Reviews (the #1 local ranking lever)
- [ ] Get your review link: GBP → "Ask for reviews" → copy link
- [ ] Send the review texts (in Content/ batch) to recent customers
- [ ] **Reply to EVERY review** (good and bad) — Google rewards engagement
- [ ] Target: a few new reviews every month, forever

## 5. Q&A + attributes + products
- [ ] Seed **Questions & Answers** yourself (ask + answer common ones: "Do you handle insurance claims?", "Are you licensed?", "Do you offer a photo audit?")
- [ ] Set attributes: "Locally owned", "Licensed", "Veteran-owned/family-owned" if applicable
- [ ] Add **Products** = your services with photos + the CPPA offer

## 6. Posts (keep it fresh)
- [ ] Post 1–2 GBP updates/week (drafts ready in Content/GBP_Posts_and_Reviews_Batch1.md + Batch2)
- [ ] Always include a photo + a Call/Learn-more button

## 7. LSA — since it's ALREADY producing (do this to get more)
- [ ] **Respond to every LSA lead within 5 minutes** — speed = you win the job
- [ ] **Dispute junk leads** in the LSA dashboard (wrong number, spam, out of area) → lowers your cost, raises your rank
- [ ] Make sure **Google Guaranteed badge** is active (business verified + license/insurance uploaded)
- [ ] Set service types + areas to match GBP exactly
- [ ] Raise the weekly budget only after speed-to-lead is dialed in

## 📊 Track it
- Watch **OpenSEO → Search Performance** for organic keyword climb.
- Watch **GBP Insights** (calls, direction requests, searches) month over month.
- Watch **LSA dashboard** (leads, cost per lead, disputes).
