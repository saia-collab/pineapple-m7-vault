# 🍍 What We Built — the simple version

## The big idea (one sentence)
We turned your computer into a **team of robot helpers** that already know your roofing business, follow your family's rules, and do the boring work — while **you stay the boss.**

**Picture your vault as a house with 4 rooms:** Command Center (the brain), Media (photos/videos), Knowledge (what we know), Tech Lab (the tools). The robots live and work in this house.

---

## What we added & fixed (and what each one DOES for you)

### 1. 🚫 We removed the old saying
- **Before:** your helpers ended everything with an old Tongan proverb.
- **You changed your slogan to "Roofing Made Sweeter."**
- **We did:** erased that old proverb from your rulebooks, your main law file, AND all **27 robot helpers**.
- **Helps:** your ads and pages now sound like the *new* you — everywhere, automatically.

### 2. 📓➡️🏠 A delivery robot for your Notebook (`/notebook-ingest`)
- **NotebookLM** is like a super-smart library in the sky. It writes you SOPs and plans — but keeps them "up there" in the cloud.
- **We built a robot** that brings those notes **down to your computer**, **cleans them** (swaps bad words — "free" becomes CPPA, "GAF" becomes IKO Certified, drops the old proverb), **files them neatly**, and only ever grabs the **new** ones (never makes doubles).
- **Helps:** all that smart research is now *yours*, tidy, on your computer — with **zero** copy-pasting by hand.

### 3. 📚 We brought in 126 notes from 3 notebooks
- Your SEO playbook, your Agent-OS manuals, and your Hermes guides.
- **Helps:** your robots can now read and answer straight from *your own* playbook.

### 4. 🛰️ We fixed your robot workers (Hermes)
- **Hermes** is a robot employee. It wears different **hats** — a roofing hat, an SEO hat, a coder hat. Each hat has a little rulebook called **soul.md**.
- **We cleaned all 27 hats' rulebooks** (took out the old proverb, added your slogan) — and **made a backup first**, so if anything looks wrong we put it back in one second.
- **Helps:** every helper now follows your *current* rules perfectly, and none can wander off-brand.

### 5. 🩺 A check-up robot (`/m7-doctor`)
- It's a **doctor for your setup.** Double-click it and it tells you: *are all your rooms there? are the helpers online? are the rules being followed?*
- **Helps:** you **never have to wonder** "is this set up right?" again — you just ask the doctor. (It already checked and said you're healthy: 27 helpers all clean, no bad colors, studio online.)

### 6. 🗺️ Little instruction booklets (so you never get lost)
- **Prompt Panel** — which helper to use + the exact words to paste.
- **Study Guide** — what each helper is and *when* to use it.
- **Hermes 101** — 6 tiny lessons to learn your best helper, one at a time.
- **AI-does / You-do map** — a chore chart: what the robots handle vs. what only you can.
- **Helps:** no more opening 20 folders looking for the right thing. You always know which button to press.

### 7. 🔍 We checked the robot-written code (with "laser eyes")
- Your notebooks also wrote little programs (`.py` / `.bat`).
- **We pulled 41, cleaned them, and scanned every one** for anything dangerous (things that delete, send, or spend). **Found zero dangerous.**
- **Helps:** you know which are safe and which to leave alone — **no accidents.**

### 8. ☁️ A magic backup (GitHub)
- After every step, we saved everything to GitHub.
- **Helps:** your notes are the **same everywhere** — this computer, your phone chats, the whole family. If a computer ever breaks, **nothing is lost.**

## 🛡️ The rule that keeps you safe (this never changes)
Every robot must **STOP and wait for you to say "GO"** before *anything* is posted, sent, or paid for. The robots write and build; **you** approve. You are always the boss. This is the **Outbox Shield.**

- **Saves time** — robots do the boring writing, checking, and filing.
- **Stops mistakes** — same rules everywhere, plus a doctor to catch problems early.
- **Easy to learn** — little booklets, one small step at a time (great when the day is busy).
- **Helps you grow** — make roofing pages, ads, and lead lists *way* faster.
- **Keeps it safe** — nothing goes public without your "yes," and everything is backed up for the family.

**Bottom line:** you spent today building a team that knows Pineapple Roofing, protects your brand, and waits for your GO — so you can spend more time on roofs and family, and less time fighting the computer. 🍍
