**📘 PM7 Playbook: Automated AI Research System**

**Goal:** Establish an auto-updating research architecture that continuously collects, organizes, and transforms high-quality data into actionable PM7 deliverables (reports, strategies, content, and competitive intelligence) while minimizing repetitive manual collection.

## **🏗️ Part 1: System Architecture & Workflow**

### **The PM7 Core Workflow**

**Identify Trusted Sources → Automate Ingestion → Organize Data → Generate Strategic Outputs → Execute Manual PM7 Review**

### **Phase 1: Notebook Setup & Source Governance**

1. **Define the Research Objective:** Assign a singular, clear focus to each notebook (e.g., *PM7 Competitor Intelligence*, *PM7 Industry Trends*, *PM7 Customer Insights*). Do not mix unrelated topics.  
2. **Curate High-Signal Sources:** Only automate trusted, verified sources (official blogs, internal PM7 drive folders, verified industry publications, trusted data feeds).  
3. **Execute Source Quality Check:** Before automation, ensure the source is original, cited, relevant, and maintains a high signal-to-noise ratio.  
4. **Establish the Automation:** Utilize your Workspace/Notebook environment integrations to automatically feed new documents, articles, or videos into the designated notebook.  
5. **Initial Sprint Test:** Run the automation for 7 days. Audit the notebook for duplicates, noise, and relevance. Adjust filters before scaling.

### **Phase 2: The PM7 Content Pipeline**

* **Step 1 \- Ingestion:** New verified material auto-populates.  
* **Step 2 \- Filtration:** AI filters noise and highlights signal.  
* **Step 3 \- Summarization:** High-level PM7 digests are generated.  
* **Step 4 \- Ideation:** Strategic content/action opportunities are identified.  
* **Step 5 \- Drafting:** V1 assets are generated.  
* **Step 6 \- PM7 Human Review:** Claims, brand alignment, and strategy are manually verified.  
* **Step 7 \- Deployment:** Approved assets go live.

**🛠️ Part 2: PM7 Master Prompts Library**

*Use these standardized prompts within your automated notebooks to generate consistent, brand-aligned outputs.*

### **1\. The PM7 Master Research Assistant**

**Prompt:** Act as my PM7 ongoing research assistant.

Research topic: \[TOPIC\]

Business/Brand: \[PM7 BRAND/CLIENT\]

Target audience: \[AUDIENCE\]

Prioritize: \[IMPORTANT TOPICS\]

Ignore: \[LOW-VALUE TOPICS\]

Whenever new sources are available:

1. Identify genuinely new information.  
2. Remove duplicates.  
3. Rank items by importance.  
4. Summarize the strongest developments.  
5. Explain why each matters to our PM7 strategy.  
6. Identify possible business opportunities.  
7. Flag anything requiring verification.  
   Use primary sources whenever possible. Never invent missing details.

### **2\. Daily PM7 Intelligence Briefing**

**Prompt:** Review the newest sources added to this notebook. Give me a daily PM7 research briefing containing:

1. The five most important developments.  
2. Why each matters strategically.  
3. Who it affects (Audience/Stakeholders).  
4. Possible business opportunities.  
5. Anything that requires independent human verification.  
   Keep the briefing concise and prioritize signal over volume.

### **3\. Weekly PM7 Industry/Competitor Report**

**Prompt:** Review all sources added during the past week. Create a structured PM7 weekly report containing:

* **Biggest Developments:** The 5 most critical updates.  
* **Emerging Trends:** Patterns backed by multiple sources.  
* **Business Impact:** What this means for \[PM7 BRAND/CLIENT\].  
* **Strategic Opportunities:** Recommended actions for next week.  
* **Verification Needed:** Claims that require manual fact-checking.  
  Rank everything by strategic importance. Avoid filler.

### **4\. PM7 Executive Decision Brief**

**Prompt:** Based on the newest research in this notebook, create a PM7 Executive Decision Brief. Include:

* What changed.  
* Why it matters to our bottom line.  
* Risk assessment (What happens if we ignore it).  
* Opportunity assessment.  
* Recommended strategic action.  
* Confidence level (based on source strength).

### **5\. Research-to-Content Engine (Multi-Channel)**

**Prompt:** Review the strongest new research in this notebook. Pick the three developments most useful to \[AUDIENCE\]. For each one create:

* One high-converting email idea.  
* One SEO-optimized article angle (Include Intent, Problem, H1, H2s, CTA).  
* One short social post idea.  
* One video hook/script outline.  
  Keep every piece strictly connected to the actual source material and aligned with PM7 brand voice.

### **6\. PM7 Customer Insights Analysis**

**Prompt:** Analyze the latest customer feedback/data ingested into this notebook. Identify:

* Recurring problems or bottlenecks.  
* Recurring sales/conversion objections.  
* Frequently requested features or services.  
* Exact language/phrasing customers repeatedly use.  
* Immediate product/service improvement opportunities.  
  Rank themes by frequency and commercial impact.

**🛡️ Part 3: PM7 Grounding & Quality Assurance**

*Automation accelerates data gathering; human intelligence ensures brand safety.*

### **The PM7 Signal-to-Noise Rule**

If fewer than 30–50% of auto-added items are useful, the automation must be paused and refined. Remove noisy sources, narrow keywords, and increase filtering parameters.

### **QA Prompt: Fact-Checking & Brand Safety**

Run this prompt before any automated research is cleared for external use or client delivery:

**Prompt:** Audit this research before public/client use. Identify and clearly label:

* Unsupported claims or hallucinated data.  
* Claims based on only one weak source.  
* Outdated information.  
* Conflicting sources.  
* Statistics that need manual verification.  
* Competitor marketing claims presented as absolute facts.

### **QA Prompt: Source Traceability**

**Prompt:** For every important claim in this document, output the supporting source URL/reference. If multiple sources support the claim, list the strongest primary source first. If a claim cannot be directly traced back to a verified source in this notebook, label it in bold: **\[PM7 VERIFICATION REQUIRED\]**.

### **QA Prompt: Fact vs. Interpretation**

**Prompt:** Review this summary and strictly label important statements using the following PM7 tags:

* **\[Confirmed Fact\]:** Supported by a primary source.  
* **\[Entity Claim\]:** Claim made by a company/competitor themselves.  
* **\[Interpretation\]:** Conclusion drawn from evidence.  
* **\[Speculation\]:** Possibility without strong confirmation.  
  Do not present speculation or entity claims as confirmed facts.
