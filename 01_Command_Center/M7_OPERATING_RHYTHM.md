# M7 — YOUR OPERATING RHYTHM (ADHD-friendly)

**The one rule:** if you do nothing else today, do the **Daily Money Loop** (15–20 min).
Everything else is bonus. One tab open (dashboard), one prompt (THE_ONLY_PROMPT), one decision at a time.

---

## ☀️ DAILY — "The Money Loop" (15–20 min, set a timer)
Do these THREE in order. This is the 80/20 of a local services business.
1. **Speed-to-lead (most important thing you do all day):** check Google LSA + Meta leads.
   Reply/call within 5 minutes. A 5-minute reply beats a perfect ad every time.
2. **Ask for reviews:** text/email yesterday's finished-job customers for a Google review.
   Reviews are the #1 lever for LSA ranking + trust. Target: 1–3 new reviews/day.
3. **Dashboard glance:** open `RUN_M7_DASHBOARD.bat` → localhost:3000. Anything to kill/scale? Note it.

> If your brain has more gas today, do ONE bonus block below. If not, you still won. Stop.

---

## 📅 WEEKLY — Monday, 60 min (one sitting)
- **Ads review:** kill creatives < 1.0% CTR, scale > 1.5% (see `SPEND_CONTROL.md`).
- **Content batch:** drop ONE idea into `03_Knowledge_Mat\raw\` → paste `THE_ONLY_PROMPT` →
  review the PAUSED drafts in `Outbox_Drafts` → schedule the week's posts.
- **Reviews scoreboard:** how many new Google reviews this week? (Target 5+.)
- **Pick next week's ONE focus business** (rotate — don't juggle all three at once).

---

## 🗓️ MONTHLY — 1st of month, 90 min
- **Local presence check** per business: Google Business Profile + LSA health + map ranking.
- **Double down:** put more budget on the channel that booked the most JOBS (not clicks).
- **Cut:** kill anything that didn't produce a booked job in 30 days.
- **Money review:** cost per booked job, per channel, per business.

---

## 🔁 BUSINESS ROTATION (so 3 businesses don't overwhelm you)
You can't push all three every day. Rotate the *weekly focus*:
- **Week 1 & 3:** Pineapple Contractors (roofing) — your core revenue engine.
- **Week 2:** Pineapple Restorations (water/mold/fire) — cross-sell from storm roofing leads.
- **Week 4:** Cowboys Supplements + merch (DTC — different motion, see note below).
Daily Money Loop still runs for whichever businesses have live leads.

> NOTE: roofing + restoration share the M7 local-services playbook (LSA, reviews, storm).
> Supplements + merch are **DTC e-commerce** — different game (email, social, offers).
> Don't force them into the same playbook; they need their own.

## 🛡️ ADHD GUARDRAILS
- ONE tab (dashboard), ONE prompt (THE_ONLY_PROMPT), ONE decision per block.
- Timer on every block. When it rings, you're done — that's a win, not a failure.
- New AI tool drops this week? Write it on a "later" list. Do NOT install it. Ship first.
- The dashboard is your external brain. If it's not on the dashboard or a task list, it doesn't exist today.
