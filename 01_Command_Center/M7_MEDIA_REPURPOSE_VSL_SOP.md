# 🎬 M7 Media Repurpose + VSL Clone SOP

Two jobs: **(A)** turn your 39GB of drone/job/testimonial media into posts that drive calls, and **(B)** a VSL (video sales letter) "clone" of you that onboards every new customer + staff member on autopilot. Both feed the same lead machine.

| Folder | What goes in |

## WORKFLOW A — raw media → posted reels (the repurpose loop)

### ✅ Free, works today (no new accounts)
1. **PICK** the clip — one strong moment (a finished roof, a drone before/after, a testimonial). Pull it from Drive.
2. **EDIT** — Studio → **Video Editor** tab → Upload → pick a brief: *"Tighten + captions"* or *"60s highlight."* FFmpeg cuts dead air + burns captions → outputs `final.mp4`. Free, local.
3. **HOOK + CAPTION** — paste to Claude/Hermes: *"Write a 9:16 reel hook (first 3 words) + caption + 5 hashtags for this clip: [Frisco hail-damage drone before/after]. Brand: free inspection, (972) 928-0788, no green. Save to Outbox."*
4. **BRAND** — add a blue/yellow lower-third + logo (Video Editor overlay, or a saved template). Keep it simple.
5. **APPROVE** — you watch the reel + read the caption → GO.
6. **NO camera? Make a FACELESS branded reel** — Studio → HyperFrames (HTML→MP4): a scripted, animated brand reel (like an explainer) with zero footage. Free, local. Good for "5 signs of hail damage" style posts.

### 🔑 Needs one login (yours, to post everywhere at once)
7. **POST** — **Blotato** (social scheduler): one clip → **IG + TikTok + Facebook + YouTube Shorts** at once, scheduled. *(Blotato account = yours to connect; posting is a GO action — drafts stay PAUSED until you approve.)*
8. **TRACK** — note which reels drive DMs/calls → make more of those.

**Cadence:** 1 reel per completed job. That alone fills your content calendar.

## WORKFLOW B — VSL clone (onboard customers + staff, repeatable)

A "clone" = your **voice + face** taught to the AI ONCE, so you can produce unlimited onboarding videos from a script — **without filming each one.**

> ▶ **Your studio already HAS this engine: `Studio → Video → The Video Director`** (build 2026-08-16). It's the "Goldie Screening Room" you saw at agentos.guide/video-studio — built in. It runs the whole flow on autopilot:
> **Brief (a topic) → Research + Script (you edit) → Avatar (HeyGen — your cloned face + voice) + B-roll (your REAL roofing clips in Workspace, or MiniMax/Grok) → Edit + Render → one MP4**, and a **judge scores it and re-cuts until it's good.** The steps below are just what that tab needs connected.
>
> **Your advantage:** the Workspace already holds ~20 real roofing videos (Highland Park Metal roofing, Frisco, Sales…). So for most videos you don't even need the AI avatar — the Director can cut YOUR footage into a captioned, music-scored promo. Use the avatar clone only for talking-head onboarding/VSL.

### Step 0 — turn on the crew's board (once)
The video crew works a live Kanban. Initialise it once in a terminal: `hermes kanban create "Pineapple video crew"` then `hermes kanban list` to confirm. The Studio → Kanban tab then mirrors it.

### Step 1 — SEED (you, once, ~10 min)
Record **one clean 60–90s talking-head** of Saia (or Naa Sione): good light, quiet room, brand backdrop, look at the camera. Say anything natural. Drop it in `02_Media_Vault/05_Onboarding_Raw/`. **This is the clone's source** — the better this clip, the better the clone.

### Step 2 — CLONE (one-time setup, your paid logins)
| Piece | Tool | Who |
|---|---|---|
| **Voice clone** | **ElevenLabs** (you have it) — upload the audio → get your AI voice | you log in once |
| **Face/avatar** | **HeyGen** or **Higgsfield** (Studio tab) — upload the clip → get your AI presenter | you log in once |
> Honest: these are paid + your accounts. I can't create logins or clone a real person's likeness for you — you set them up. Once done, they're reusable forever.

### Step 3 — SCRIPT (free, me/Hermes)
Paste: *"Write a 60-second VSL onboarding script for a new Pineapple Roofing customer: welcome, what happens on the free inspection, the storm damage report they keep, IKO install, next step (972) 928-0788. Warm, local, brand law. Save to Outbox."* → you approve.

### Step 4 — GENERATE (the repeat)
Feed the approved script to your voice+avatar clone → it renders the video **in your face/voice.** Change the script → new video. **That's the "repeat":** same clone, new script = new onboarding video, no re-filming.

### Step 5 — USE (the whole point)
- **New customer** → auto-send the "Welcome + what to expect on your inspection" VSL (builds trust before you arrive).
- **New staff** → a training-video library (safety, brand, process) from the same clone.
- **Landing page** → drop the VSL on the site as the sales video.
- **Objection-handlers** → one 30s VSL each ("Is the inspection really free?", "How does the claim work?") → send on demand.

### Step 6 — BRAND + CAPTION + POST
Same as Workflow A steps 4–7 (lower-third, captions, Blotato).
