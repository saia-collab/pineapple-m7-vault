2. *Zero-Stress claim (outcome):* "We photograph the damage. The insurer approves the claim. Your CPPA costs nothing."
3. *Local trust (social proof):* "350+ North Texas families trusted Pineapple after a storm — 20+ years, IKO Certified, RCAT #03-0637."

> Here's what we do: (1) photograph everything — drone + ground, (2) document every damage point, (3) hand you an insurance-ready report they accept.
> Cost? Your CPPA is complimentary. Obligation? None.

**Creative:** before/after hail damage (dents, lifted shingles, granule loss). Navy hero + Gold CTA. Overlay: "Hidden Roof Damage? Book Your CPPA." Video: drone neighborhood → close-up damage → homeowner relief.

**Targeting:** Frisco 75033/75034 · Lewisville 75067/75068 · Plano 75023/75024/75074 · McKinney 75069/75070 · Dallas core. Age 35–65, homeowners. Interests: Homeowners Insurance, Home Repair, Insurance Claims, Home Improvement. Lookalike: past customers. Exclude renters + roofs replaced <2 yrs.
