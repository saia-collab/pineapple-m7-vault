# 📈 M7 SEO + LSA — The Weekly Ranking Loop

> GSC is connected (OpenSEO → Search Performance). This is the repeatable loop to
> turn "almost ranking" into page-1 clicks — mostly FREE, run by your fleet.

## 🎯 THE CORE IDEA
Google already shows Pineapple for 100+ searches (positions 5–20 = "striking
distance"). Moving ONE of those from position 12 → 4 can multiply its clicks.
We don't guess keywords — GSC tells us exactly which pages are closest.

---

## 🔁 THE WEEKLY LOOP (30–45 min/week)

**STEP 1 — READ (you, 5 min)** · *OpenSEO → Search Performance → Striking distance*
- Sort by Impressions (high impressions + position 6–15 = biggest wins).
- Pick 2–3 targets. Note the **query** + the **page** it's tied to.
- Example this week: "grapevine roofing company" (114 impr, pos 16) →
  /service-areas/tarrant-county-tx page.

**STEP 2 — WRITE (Hermes, free)** · *Hermes → Goal Mode*
Paste (swap in your target):
```
caveman: Profile SEO. We rank position 16 for "grapevine roofing company" on our
tarrant-county service-area page. Rewrite/expand that page to win the top 3:
stronger title+H1 with the keyword, FAQ, local trust signals (RCAT #03-0637, IKO
Certified, since 2005), internal links. CPPA (never "free"), zero green. Save to
01_Command_Center/Outbox_Drafts/SEO_Posts/. PAUSED.
```

**STEP 3 — REVIEW (you)** · *Outbox_Drafts/* → read it, tweak, approve.

**STEP 4 — DEPLOY (you)** · *Dashboard → Deploy tab* → push the updated page live.

**STEP 5 — TRACK (weekly)** · *OpenSEO → Search Performance*
- Watch that query's **position** climb over 2–4 weeks.
- CTR up + position down = winning. Then pick the next target. Repeat forever.

## 📞 LSA (Local Services Ads) — separate from SEO
LSA = the "Google Guaranteed" pay-per-lead ads at the very top of local search.
This is NOT automated by the dashboard — it's a Google signup + verification the
business owner does once:

1. Go to **google.com/localservices** (or ads.google.com/local-services-ads),
   sign in as **marketing@pineapplecontractors.com**.
2. Confirm **Roofing** + service areas (DFW / Frisco / Tarrant / Collin / Denton).
3. Pass **Google Guaranteed screening**: business + owner background check,
   upload **RCAT License #03-0637** + liability insurance.
4. Set a **weekly budget** and service types (repair, replacement, storm).
5. Go live → leads come in by phone/message; you pay per lead (dispute junk leads
   in the LSA dashboard).

**Tracking LSA:** the LSA dashboard shows leads + cost. Feed won leads into the
M7 lead engine (`M7_LEAD_ENGINE.md`) for speed-to-lead follow-up + review asks.

> Honest note: the dashboard can't run/verify LSA for you — it's a Google process.
> But SEO (above) is fully in your control and free. Do SEO weekly; set up LSA once.
