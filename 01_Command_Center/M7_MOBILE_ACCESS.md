# M7 — WORK FROM YOUR PHONE (mobile + desktop, one playbook)

## The key idea
- **The PLAYBOOK (the .md knowledge) travels.** It lives in Google Drive, so your phone can read/edit it.
- **The ENGINE (scripts, dashboard, Ollama, firewall) stays on the desktop.** Phones can't run those.
- So: on mobile you **think, draft, capture, and dispatch**; the desktop **executes**.

## 1. See/edit the playbook on your phone (pick ONE)
- **Easiest — Google Drive app:** open the `Pineapple Contractors M7` folder → read any `.md`
  (MASTER_PLAYBOOK, STUDY_GUIDE, QUICK_CARD). Good for reading on the go.
- **Best for editing — Obsidian mobile + sync:** install Obsidian on your phone. To open the
  SAME vault, use one sync method:
  - **Obsidian Sync** (paid, easiest, most reliable cross-device), OR
  - keep the vault inside a phone-synced Drive/Dropbox folder and open it as a vault.
  > Note: Google Drive "Computers/My PC" backup is one-way and not ideal for live mobile editing —
  > Obsidian Sync avoids the headaches.

## 2. Draft on the go with Claude mobile
- Use the **Claude app** on your phone in this same Project. The Project carries your context
  (brand rules, avatars, CPPA language). Draft hooks/copy there, then paste into `Outbox_Drafts`
  on desktop later — or have Claude Code file it next session.

## 3. "Dispatch" — fire a task from your phone, desktop runs it
Two clean options:
- **Scheduled tasks:** anything on a cadence (daily briefing, telemetry refresh) is already handled
  by `M7_DAILY_SYNC` on the desktop — no phone needed.
- **Hermes remote dispatch:** Hermes can take a goal from a phone-friendly channel (Telegram/email
  per the Hermes SOP) and execute it on the always-on desktop. Set this up once when you want true
  "text my OS a task" capability. Everything still lands PAUSED (Outbox Shield).

## Reality check (so you're not frustrated)
- You can't run the dashboard or launch ads "from the phone" directly — and you shouldn't want to;
  publishing is the one deliberate human gate.
- The realistic mobile workflow: **capture ideas + review drafts on phone → publish from desktop.**
