Insurance Claim FAQ

Is hail damage covered by insurance in Texas?

Covered storm damage is evaluated by your carrier on evidence. Our CPPA provides comprehensive documentation for a successful claim.

Are you a public adjuster?

No. We are an IKO Certified roofing contractor (RCAT #03-0637). We provide the engineering-grade documentation your carrier needs — we do not negotiate the claim for you.

What does it cost me?
