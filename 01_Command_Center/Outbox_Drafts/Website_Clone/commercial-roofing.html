Common Questions

Commercial roofing, answered

Can you work without shutting down our business?

Yes. We phase the work, stage materials off-hours where needed, and coordinate access so your operation keeps running. Your schedule drives ours.

Should we re-roof or just repair?

That depends on the membrane's age, moisture in the insulation, and the deck condition. The free roof inspection gives you a documented answer — repair, recover, or full tear-off — with the reasoning behind it.

Do you handle insurance and storm claims for commercial roofs?

Yes. We document damage with photos and written scope, meet your adjuster on site, and manage the claim with you from first inspection to final invoice.

What warranties come with a commercial system?

Manufacturer system warranties (typically 15–30 years depending on the membrane) plus The Pineapple Standard workmanship commitment. We register every warranty for you.

Do you service multifamily and portfolios?

Yes — from a single property to a full portfolio, with consolidated reporting and maintenance scheduling across sites.
