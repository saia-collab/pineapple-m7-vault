Common Questions

Restoration, answered

How fast can you respond to water or storm damage?

We run a 24/7 response line. The faster we're on site, the more damage we prevent — call (972) 928-0788 any time and we'll dispatch as quickly as possible.

Do you work with my insurance company?

Yes. We document everything with photos, moisture readings and a written scope, meet your adjuster on site, and manage the claim alongside you so you're never negotiating alone.

Do you rebuild, or just clean up?

Both. We're a licensed general contractor (RCAT #03-0637), so we handle mitigation, remediation and the full rebuild — drywall, flooring, paint and finishes.

How do you handle mold safely?

We contain the affected area, remove impacted materials safely, remediate, and — critically — fix the moisture source so the mold doesn't come back.

What does the CPPA cover for restoration?

A documented photo assessment of the damage, moisture mapping where relevant, and a clear plan for mitigation and rebuild — at no cost and no obligation.
