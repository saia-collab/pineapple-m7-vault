Common Questions

Residential roofing, answered

How do I know if I need a repair or a full replacement?

That depends on age, the extent of damage and what's underneath. A free roof inspection is exactly how we figure that out — you'll get a documented read on the current condition and an honest recommendation, not a sales pitch.

Are you actually local to DFW?

Yes. We've been based in Frisco, Texas since 2021 — RCAT License #03-0637, IKO Certified, and the same local ownership for 20+ years. We're not a storm-chasing crew that disappears after the next hail event.

What does "IKO Certified" actually mean for my roof?

It means our installers are trained and audited by the manufacturer on IKO's full shingle system. The install meets IKO's spec — which is what unlocks the 50-year product warranty and our upgraded workmanship coverage.

Do you handle insurance claims?

Yes. We document the damage with photos and written scope, meet with your adjuster on site, and walk the claim alongside you. You're never arguing with the insurance company alone.

How long does a typical residential re-roof take?

Most single-family homes in DFW are completed in 1–3 days once the crew is on site, weather permitting. We give you a schedule up front and keep you updated daily.

What does the free roof inspection actually include?

A complete photo audit of the roof, attic ventilation, gutters and visible flashings — and a documented read on what we see, what's urgent and what can wait. No cost, no obligation.
