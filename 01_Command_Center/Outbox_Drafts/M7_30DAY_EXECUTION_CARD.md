## ✂️ BULK CUT-UPS — the honest answer
**There is no "batch 20 videos" button.** Here's what's real in your build:

| Tool | What it does | Bulk? |
|---|---|---|
| **Video Editor** tab | one clip → trimmed + captioned | one at a time (~5 min each) |
| **OpenMontage** tab | stitches multiple clips into a montage | multi-clip, but one output |
| **Hermes** | ❌ has NO video tools (files/browser/code only) | can't cut video |

**The realistic "bulk" method — a 30-minute batch session:**
1. Open **Video Editor**.
2. Drop clip #1 → paste: *"Cut to the strongest 20 seconds. Remove dead air and umms. Burn bold 2-word captions."*
3. While it renders, **queue the next clip**.
4. Repeat ×5. **5 clips ≈ 30 minutes = a full week of reels.**
5. Save all outputs to Drive `04_FINAL_FOR_BLATATO/`.

**Do 5 clips a week, not 30 in a day.** That's how it stays sustainable.
