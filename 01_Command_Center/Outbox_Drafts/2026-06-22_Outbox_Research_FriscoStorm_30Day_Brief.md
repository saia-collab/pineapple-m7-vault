   — Not answered. Commercial PMs ask this every time.

   — Not answered in any FAQ; relevant to the $700K+ estate and commercial avatars.
7. **"Can you work directly with my property management company's preferred vendor onboarding?"**
   — Partially addressed in §5.4 (priority scheduling, dedicated coordinator) but no concrete
     vendor-onboarding answer.
8. **"What is your warranty on labor vs. manufacturer warranty on materials?"**
   — IKO manufacturer warranty referenced; labor warranty not quantified.
