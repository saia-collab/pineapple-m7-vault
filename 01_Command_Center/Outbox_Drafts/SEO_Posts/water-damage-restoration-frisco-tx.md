## What To Do Before We Arrive

A few calm steps can save thousands in damage.

Shut off the water source if you can reach it safely.

Cut power to affected areas only if it is safe to do so.

Move valuables and documents up and out of the wet zone.

Do not run a household vacuum over standing water.

Then call a professional restoration team, because drying a structure properly is not the same as mopping a floor.
