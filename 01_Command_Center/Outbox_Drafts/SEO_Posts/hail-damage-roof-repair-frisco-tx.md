## Step Two: Understand What Hail Actually Does To a Roof

Hail damage is not one thing, and knowing the layers helps you ask better questions.

Here is what our crews look for on a Frisco roof after a storm.

| Layer | What hail does | Why it matters |
|-------|----------------|----------------|
| Granules | Knocked loose on impact | Exposes asphalt to UV, ages the shingle fast |
| Shingle mat | Bruised or fractured | Breaks the water seal even with no visible hole |
| Flashing & vents | Dented or loosened | Creates entry points around penetrations |
| Decking | Soft spots from repeated hits | Signals deeper, structural moisture risk |

A repair that only replaces a few surface shingles but ignores bruised mat and loosened flashing is a repair that fails again.

The Pineapple Standard is to evaluate every layer, not just the one you can see.
