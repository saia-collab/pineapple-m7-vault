## The commercial storm damage roof inspection playbook (6 steps)

### Step 1 — Storm date and soft-metal test panel

The morning after a North Texas hailstorm, the property manager's first call should be to Pineapple at **972-928-0788**. We do not need to be on the roof to start. We need the **storm date** and access to the **soft-metal test panels** on the property: HVAC fins, coping, gutters, downspouts, condensate lines, antenna mounts, and refrigerant lines. Soft-metal test panels are the carrier's primary hail-impact indicator on a commercial roof. We photograph them, log the impacts, and correlate to the NWS hail swath for the date.

### Step 2 — Drone overflight and sectioned photography

Pineapple's CPPA includes a drone overflight of the entire roof footprint. We photograph every section, every penetration, every seam transition, and every repair patch from above. The drone set is geo-tagged to the section labels in the line-item scope so the carrier's adjuster can match scope to photo without a ladder.

### Step 3 — Infrared moisture scan

Flat and low-slope roofs fail in the **decking and insulation**, not the membrane. Pineapple uses an infrared moisture scan to identify wet insulation and trapped moisture. Wet insulation is a code-required replacement, not a patch. The scan is the artifact that converts a "no damage observed" denial into a "decking and insulation entitlement" line item on the carrier's scope.

### Step 4 — Close-up and seam photography

After the drone and the scan, our team walks every seam and every penetration with a close-up camera. TPO seam micro-fissures, modified-bitumen cap cracks, and coping joint separations are photographed at a scale the carrier's adjuster can re-photograph on inspection. This is the documentation that moves the conversation.

### Step 5 — Hail-swath overlay and storm correlation

Every Pineapple CPPA includes a **hail-swath overlay** from the National Weather Service storm report for the property's storm date. The overlay is plotted on the property's roof plan. The correlation is the answer to the carrier's most common denial: "we cannot confirm hail at the property address on the storm date." The overlay removes the question.

### Step 6 — Executive summary, line-item scope, and CPPA deliverable

The CPPA deliverable for a commercial or multi-unit property includes:

- **Photograph set** — drone, close-up, and soft-metal test panel.
- **Infrared moisture scan report** — wet insulation flagged by section.
- **Hail-swath overlay** — NWS-storm correlated.
- **Written executive summary** — one page the asset manager can attach to the loss notice.
- **Line-item scope** — scope of work, material callout, and code-required upgrades.
- **Insurance-grade documentation** — comprehensive documentation for a successful claim.
