It's one of the most common questions North Texas homeowners ask us: should I go with shingles or metal? Both are excellent choices — the right one depends on your budget, how long you plan to stay, and the look you want. Here's an honest breakdown.

## Cost
**Shingles** are the more budget-friendly option up front and fit the majority of DFW homes. **Metal** costs more to install, but spreads that cost over a much longer lifespan. If you're staying in the home long-term, the math often favors metal.

## Lifespan
- **Architectural shingles:** roughly 15–25 years in the North Texas climate.
- **Standing-seam metal:** roughly 40–70 years.

A quality metal roof can genuinely be the last roof you ever buy.

## Hail and wind performance
This matters a lot here. Both can be rated for impact — **Class 4 impact-rated shingles** perform very well and can even lower your insurance premium. **Metal** also handles hail and high winds excellently, though large hail can dent some panels cosmetically. For most homeowners, either option stands up to North Texas storms when installed correctly.

## Energy efficiency
**Metal** reflects heat and can reduce summer cooling costs — a real advantage through a Texas July. **Shingles** have improved with reflective and "cool roof" options, but metal still leads on energy performance.

## Curb appeal
**Shingles** offer a classic look with a huge range of colors and profiles, including designer options. **Metal** gives a modern, distinctive look and is increasingly popular for accents and full roofs on higher-end homes.

## So which should you choose?
| | Shingles | Metal |
|---|---|---|
| Up-front cost | Lower | Higher |
| Lifespan | 15–25 yrs | 40–70 yrs |
| Energy savings | Good | Best |
| Best for | Most homes, tighter budgets | Long-term owners, modern look |

There's no wrong answer — only the right fit for *your* home, roof slope and budget.
