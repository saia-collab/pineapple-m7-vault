# 5 Signs of Hidden Hail Damage on a Frisco Roof

Your roof can take a beating during a Frisco hailstorm and still look fine from the ground. Hidden damage often goes unnoticed for months — until an interior leak or a failed insurance claim forces your hand. Here are five signs that your roof may have taken storm damage that deserves a closer look.

---

## 1. Granules in the Gutters and Downspouts

After a storm, grab a ladder and check your gutters. If you find a steady stream of sand-like granules — enough to coat the bottom of the gutter — it is a strong indicator that hail has knocked the protective layer off your shingles. A light dusting after a new roof is normal; heavy granule loss after a storm is not.

## 2. Dented or Bruised Shingles Visible from a Ladder

Walk the perimeter with a flashlight and look at the shingles up close. Hail leaves faint dimples, bruising, or dark spots where the mat underneath has been compromised. These soft spots are not always visible from the street, but they shorten the life of the shingle and open the door to water intrusion.

## 3. Cracked, Split, or Missing Shingles

Even small hailstones traveling at highway speeds can crack asphalt shingles, especially if they are a few years old. Walk around the roof edge and look for shingles that are split along the grain, cracked through the middle, or simply gone. One missing shingle can expose the underlayment to wind-driven rain.

## 4. Damaged Metal Flashing, Vents, and Fascia

Hail does not discriminate — it hits everything on the roof. Check the metal drip edge, vent boots, plumbing vent collars, and metal fascia for dents, scratches, or loose seams. If the flashing is dented, the seal around it may be broken, creating a leak path that shows up inside the attic long after the storm passes.

## 5. Spots on the Attic Ceiling or New Water Stains

The most convincing sign of hidden roof damage is water where it should not be. Check the attic after a rainstorm for fresh stains on the underside of the deck, damp insulation, or drips on the rafters. If you see water marks on the ceiling below the attic, the roof is letting moisture in — and hail damage may be part of the reason.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long after a hailstorm should I inspect my roof?**
Within a week is ideal. Some signs — like new ceiling stains — appear quickly, while others — like loose granules and dented flashing — are easier to spot before debris or weather obscures them.

**Can hidden hail damage really lead to a leak months later?**
Yes. A bruised shingle or a cracked seal around a vent may hold up through a few rainfalls, then fail when the next storm hits. The damage compounds quietly until moisture finds its way inside.

**Is a roof inspection worth it if my roof looks fine from the ground?**
Absolutely. Most hail damage is hidden from street-level view. A professional storm damage report documents what your eyes cannot see — before a small problem becomes a costly repair.
