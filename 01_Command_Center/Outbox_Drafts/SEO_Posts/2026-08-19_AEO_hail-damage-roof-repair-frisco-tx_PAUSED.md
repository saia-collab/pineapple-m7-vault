## What does hail damage to a roof look like in Frisco?
Most hail damage is not visible from the ground. On a Frisco asphalt-shingle roof it shows up as **granule loss** (bare black spots where the surface has worn away), **soft bruises** you can feel but rarely see, **cracked or broken seal strips**, and **dents on gutters, vents, and downspouts** that confirm the storm. Because the bruising fractures the mat *under* the granules, a roof can look fine from the driveway and still be failing.

## How do I know if I need repair or full replacement?
A free roof inspection tells you which you need — you see the photos and the scope, then you decide. As a general rule:
- **Repair** — isolated damage on a newer roof, a few slopes, no widespread mat fracture.
- **Replacement** — hail bruising across most slopes, an older roof, or damage the manufacturer won't warranty as a spot fix.
