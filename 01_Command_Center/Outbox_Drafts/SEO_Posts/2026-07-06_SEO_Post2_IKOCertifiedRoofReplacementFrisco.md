## Why "IKO Certified" is not a marketing sticker

The shingle industry in North America is a long, complicated value chain. The manufacturer makes the product. A distributor stocks it. A roofing company buys it. A crew installs it. The homeowner hopes it lasts.

The certification layer sits between the manufacturer and the installer. **IKO Certified** is a credential issued by IKO — a publicly traded North American shingle manufacturer with North Texas distribution — that requires four things from the contractor:

1. **Documented training** on the full IKO product line and installation standards.
2. **Audited install history** on past projects.
3. **Proof of insurance** at manufacturer-required limits.
4. **Quality control participation** — IKO reviews a sample of the contractor's installs.

A non-certified contractor can still buy IKO shingles at the lumber yard. The homeowner gets the material warranty — the shingle's warranty against manufacturing defects. The homeowner does **not** get the system warranty — the manufacturer's commitment that the **installed system** (shingles + underlayment + flashing + ventilation + workmanship) will perform for the warranty term.

That gap is the whole point of an IKO Certified roof replacement in Frisco TX.

## What the IKO Certified install actually looks like on a Frisco roof

A Frisco IKO Certified roof replacement by Pineapple Contractors is not the same workflow as a non-certified install. The differences are small per step and large in aggregate.

### Step 1 — free roof inspection before the estimate

The project starts with a **free roof inspection** — drone and close-up photography of every slope, every valley, every penetration. We do not estimate a re-roof from the driveway. The free roof inspection captures the actual condition of decking, flashing, ventilation, and code-required upgrades so the Full Restoration Coverage Evaluation is accurate, not aspirational.

### Step 2 — Decking inspection at tear-off

When the existing layers come off, the decking is exposed. This is the moment a non-certified crew says "good enough" and re-sheathes over questionable boards. An IKO Certified install requires documentation of the decking condition. Soft spots, water damage, and code-deficient spans are photographed, replaced to current IRC standards, and the manufacturer is notified. This is the step that protects the system warranty for the next 30+ years.

### Step 3 — Code-upgraded underlayment

North Texas hail, heat, and UV punish underlayment. The 2024 IRC update (effective in Collin County) tightened underlayment requirements for re-roofs in hail-prone swaths. Pineapple Contractors installs **ice-and-water shield** at every valley, eave, and penetration regardless of code minimum, and **synthetic underlayment** across the field. This is the detail that separates a value-engineered install from a cheap install.

### Step 4 — IKO product line — Cambridge, Dynasty, or Nordic

For most Frisco homes, Pineapple recommends:

- **IKO Cambridge®** — architectural, 30-year limited lifetime. Value-engineered for production homes and rental properties.
- **IKO Dynasty®** — Class 3 impact resistance, 50-year limited lifetime. The default for hail-prone Frisco swaths.
- **IKO Nordic™** — Class 4 impact resistance, the highest IKO impact rating. Specified for Starwood, Newman Village, and other luxury enclaves where insurance discount eligibility matters.

The right shingle depends on the property's hail-swath history, slope, ventilation, and the homeowner's insurance posture. We do not push one product. We publish the matrix and let the homeowner decide.

### Step 5 — Flashing, ventilation, and code-required upgrades

This is where the audit catches the bid-peddler. A non-certified crew reuses existing flashing, skips the drip edge upgrade, and adds a single roof vent "for circulation." An IKO Certified install at Pineapple includes new flashing at every penetration, code-compliant drip edge, and a ventilation calculation tied to attic square footage. The install matches the manufacturer spec that backs the warranty.

### Step 6 — Final photo audit and warranty registration

When the install is done, Pineapple does a **second free roof inspection** — a final photo audit of every slope, every flashing, every penetration. That photo set is the documentation that registers the system warranty with IKO. The homeowner gets the warranty certificate, the photo set, and the manufacturer's confirmation in writing.
