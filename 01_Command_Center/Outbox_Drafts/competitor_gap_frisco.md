## Methodology and scope

- Search captured: 2026-07-22 at approximately 4:40 PM CDT.
- Ranking source: Yahoo US organic results for the exact query. Google returned an anti-automation interstitial from the research environment, so no Google rank position is claimed.
- Selection: the first five unique local contractor pages in organic order; directories and duplicate marketplace results were excluded.
- Word count: approximate rendered words in the page’s main element, or body when no main element existed; scripts, styles, templates, and non-rendered code were excluded.
- H2s: rendered H2 text in page order. Competitor phrases conflicting with the M7 lexicon were normalized rather than reproduced.
- Schema: JSON-LD types detected in the fetched HTML.
- Collection method: each URL was fetched once with a standard desktop browser user agent; final redirect URL and HTTP 200 status were verified before extraction.
- Mention method: case-insensitive counts were taken from rendered main/body text for hail, storm, insurance, claim, documentation, document, and adjuster. Counts are directional page-audit signals, not keyword-density recommendations.
- Limitation: rankings can vary by engine, location, device, and time. This is a timestamped contractor-page snapshot, not a universal rank claim.

## The five gaps Pineapple Roofing can own

### 1. Make the CPPA a visible evidence product, not a generic inspection promise

None of the five pages clearly shows the complete artifact a homeowner or property manager receives. Own this with a redacted sample CPPA containing annotated photographs, roof-plane map, observed-condition legend, material notes, priority levels, and next-step checklist. Redact the owner’s name, street number, claim number, policy data, signatures, image metadata, and any identifying interior views; preserve the document structure so visitors can judge its usefulness. Answer the query in the first 40 words and make “what you receive” a dedicated H2.

### 2. Publish the definitive Frisco hail-and-claim documentation workflow

Competitors mention insurance, but no page fully explains the sequence from storm date to site photography, adjuster meeting preparation, scope comparison, material identification, supplement-ready records, and homeowner archive. Build a factual timeline and checklist focused on comprehensive documentation for a successful claim without promising coverage or claim outcomes.

### 3. Create separate paths for estate owners and multi-property managers

The ranking pages mostly speak to a generic homeowner. Own high-value intent with distinct sections for Frisco estate roofing and commercial portfolio roofing: access planning, landscaping protection, resident or tenant communication, phased scheduling, daily photo logs, manufacturer specifications, and closeout records. This matches Pineapple Roofing’s premium audience without mixing in Pineapple Restorations terminology.

### 4. Become the source for neighborhood-level Frisco roofing evidence

HonestRoof lists neighborhoods and ZIP codes, but the field lacks original, citation-worthy local evidence. Publish dated, verified Frisco case studies and storm-response explainers for 75033, 75034, and 75035, including roof system, observed conditions, documented process, project timeline, and labeled photographs. Add clean RoofingContractor, Service, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList, PostalAddress, AggregateRating, and area-served schema only where page content supports it.

### 5. Build the strongest trust-and-answer layer in the SERP

The competitors separate credentials, FAQs, reviews, and CTAs instead of combining them into direct answers. Pineapple can place a concise answer block, Frisco-specific FAQs, verified review excerpts, and a CPPA CTA beside the full trust stack: RCAT License #03-0637 · IKO Certified · 5-Star · Since 2005 · 972-928-0788.
