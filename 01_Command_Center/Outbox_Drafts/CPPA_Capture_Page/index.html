Straight answers

Before we step on your roof.

What is a free roof inspection?

A free roof inspection is a drone and ground roof inspection that produces a photographic record of visible storm damage. You keep the report whether or not you move forward with Pineapple.

Can damage exist before a leak appears?

Yes. Hail and wind can weaken roofing materials without an immediate interior leak. Early documentation helps establish what happened and when it happened.

Do you work with insurance carriers?

Yes. We organize the inspection evidence and can meet your adjuster to help make sure documented damage and the repair scope are clearly presented. Coverage decisions remain with your carrier under your policy.

What if my claim was already denied?

We can inspect and re-document the property, then help you understand whether there is evidence worth presenting for reconsideration. A new audit does not guarantee a changed coverage decision.

Where do you work?

Dallas-Fort Worth and surrounding North Texas communities, including Frisco, Lewisville, Plano, McKinney, Allen, and nearby cities.
