> **PAUSED — your sales playbook.** Brand law: CPPA (never "free"), IKO, Full Restoration Coverage (never "$0 down"), RCAT #03-0637, 972-928-0788.

# 🤝 PINEAPPLE SALES SYSTEM — close without being pushy

**Selling isn't convincing.** It's showing someone the gap between their roof now (maybe damaged, claim window
closing) and their roof after (new, paid by insurance). You just walk them across that gap.

---

## 🗣️ THE 60-SECOND OPENER (door OR phone)
> "Hi, I'm [name] with Pineapple Roofing — we're the crew that just documented [neighbor's/a nearby] roof
> after the last storm. I'm not here to sell you anything. A lot of homes on this street took hail damage you
> can't see from the ground, and there's a window to document it before insurance stops covering it. It takes
> me 20 minutes to fly a drone and check yours — you keep the report either way. Want me to take a quick look?"

Why it works: names a real neighbor (proof), removes pressure ("not here to sell"), gives a reason (the
window), and the ask is tiny (20 minutes, you keep the report).

---

## ❓ 5 DISCOVERY QUESTIONS (find the real worry)
1. "How old is the roof — and has it been through a hail storm since it went up?"
2. "Have you filed a roof claim before, or would this be your first time?"
3. "What's your biggest worry when it comes to dealing with insurance on this?"
4. "If there IS damage, would you rather it get handled now, or wait and risk paying out of pocket later?"
5. "Who else helps you make a call like this — is it just you, or you and your spouse?"

The real fear is usually one of these: *a denied claim, a surprise bill, or their rates going up.* Listen for it.

## 🔑 THE CLOSER SCRIPT (once you're on the roof/report)
- **C — Clarify:** "So the main thing is you want the damage handled without a big out-of-pocket bill, right?"
- **L — Label the problem:** "Here's the issue — this hail bruising will keep letting water in, and insurers
  deny it once it's too old to prove. That's the clock we're beating."
- **O — Overview the path:** "Here's how it works: I document everything today, we file with proof, I meet
  your adjuster and defend the scope, and we install an IKO Certified roof. You pay your deductible — that's it."
- **S — Sell the outcome (not features):** "You end up with a brand-new roof, a clean claim, and your home
  protected — for the cost of your deductible instead of $15k–$25k."
- **E — Explain the guarantee:** "If there's no documentable damage, you owe nothing and keep the report. And
  our workmanship is guaranteed — if it's not installed to IKO spec, we make it right."
- **R — Reinforce with honest urgency:** "The only real deadline is your claim window — the longer we wait,
  the harder it is to prove. Want me to lock in your CPPA now?"

## 🛡️ TOP 10 OBJECTIONS — exact words to say
1. **"I need to think about it."** → "Totally fair. What specifically do you want to think through — the
   process, the cost, or whether the damage is real? Let's clear that up right now so you're not guessing later."
2. **"My insurance already said no."** → "That's the most common reason we get called. Most denials are from
   weak documentation, not no damage. We re-document it properly and reopen it — that's literally our specialty."
3. **"I want to get 3 quotes."** → "Smart — get them. But this isn't a quote, it's a documented claim. The
   other guys quote a price; we prove the damage so insurance pays. Keep the report and compare."
4. **"Will my rates go up?"** → "A storm claim is an 'act of God' claim — it's not the same as an at-fault
   claim, and it's spread across everyone in your area, not just you. Most homeowners see no change."
5. **"Is this going to cost me anything?"** → "The audit is complimentary — you keep the report. If we move
   forward, insurance funds the approved scope and your cost is just your deductible."
6. **"I need to talk to my spouse."** → "Of course — this is a both-of-you decision. Let's get the audit done
   so you're bringing them proof, not a maybe. When can we all hop on for 5 minutes?"
7. **"I already have a roofer."** → "Great — keep them. This is just documentation. If there's damage, you'll
   want proof no matter who installs it. You've got nothing to lose and a report to gain."
8. **"How do I know the damage is real?"** → "You won't take my word for it — that's the whole point. The drone
   + ground photos show you exactly what I see. If there's nothing, I'll tell you, and you owe nothing."
9. **"I don't have time right now."** → "It's 20 minutes and you don't have to be on the roof — I'll send you
   the report. What's worse, 20 minutes now or a denied claim in six months?"
10. **"Just send me a price."** → "I can't price a roof I haven't documented — that's guessing, and guessing is
    how homeowners overpay. Let me document it (complimentary), then you'll have real numbers."

## ⏰ HONEST URGENCY (never fake it)
The clock is real: **the longer after a storm, the harder damage is to prove, and claim windows close.** That's
your only urgency — and it's true. Don't invent fake discounts.
