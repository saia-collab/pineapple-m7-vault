# 🏆 SEO PLAYBOOK — Family Training

**What this room does:** finds the Google searches we ALMOST rank for, and pushes them to the top so the phone rings.

## ✈️ PRE-FLIGHT CHECKLIST (do before running anything)
- [ ] Search Console is open and connected.
- [ ] You know this week's target city (Frisco / Plano / Lewisville).
- [ ] Brand law in your head: never "free" → free roof inspection. Never "GAF" → IKO Certified. No green.
- [ ] Everything you make lands PAUSED in Outbox. Nobody publishes without Saia's GO.

## 🪜 THE STEPS (do them in order)
1. Open Search Console → Performance.
2. Sort by Impressions. Find keywords at position 5–20. That's the money zone.
3. Those are "so close." Pick the top 3.
4. Hand them to Claude: "strengthen the page for [keyword]."
5. Review the draft. Check brand law. Hit GO only when it's clean.
6. That's it. One loop. Repeat weekly.

**The rule:** you don't need 100 keywords. You need the 3 you almost own. Win those. Then the next 3.
