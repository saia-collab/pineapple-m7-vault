## What storm damage actually looks like in Frisco
A trained inspector checks for the damage untrained eyes miss:

- **Hail bruising** — soft, dark spots where granules are knocked off, exposing the mat. Often invisible from the ground.
- **Wind-lifted or missing shingles** — especially along ridges and edges.
- **Damaged flashing** — around chimneys, vents and valleys, where leaks start.
- **Granules in the gutters** — a sign the surface is wearing prematurely.
