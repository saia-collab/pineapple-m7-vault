## What drives the cost of a Southlake roof replacement

Roofing quotes vary for a handful of concrete reasons. Here's what actually moves the number:

- **Roof size (square footage).** Priced per "square" (100 sq ft). A larger Southlake home means more material and labor.
- **Pitch and complexity.** Steep pitches, multiple valleys, dormers, and chimneys take longer and require more safety setup.
- **Material choice.** Architectural asphalt (IKO) is the value sweet spot; standing-seam metal and tile cost more but last decades longer.
- **Tear-off vs. overlay.** A proper tear-off down to the deck (what we recommend) costs more than laying over old shingles — but it's the only way to inspect and repair the decking.
- **Decking repair.** Rotted or storm-damaged decking is replaced before the new roof goes on. This is found on tear-off, not before.
- **Insurance vs. out-of-pocket.** If hail or wind caused the damage, **Full Restoration Coverage** through your policy can change your net cost dramatically.

## Roofing materials Southlake homeowners choose

- **Architectural asphalt shingles (IKO Certified):** the most popular choice — a ~30-year service life, strong wind ratings, and the best cost-to-value in North Texas.
- **Impact-resistant (Class 4) shingles:** built for hail country; may qualify you for an insurance premium discount.
- **Standing-seam metal:** 50-year lifespan, excellent in heat and hail, higher upfront cost.
- **Tile / slate:** premium look and longevity for the right Southlake home; heavier and pricier to install.

## Is your roof storm-damaged? (Southlake sits in hail country)

North Texas takes some of the heaviest hail in the country, and Southlake is squarely in it. Hail and wind damage is often **not visible from the ground** — bruised shingles, granule loss, and cracked seals shorten your roof's life quietly. If your roof is 10+ years old or you've had a recent storm, a free roof inspection tells you whether you're looking at a repair, a replacement, or a valid insurance claim under **Full Restoration Coverage**.
