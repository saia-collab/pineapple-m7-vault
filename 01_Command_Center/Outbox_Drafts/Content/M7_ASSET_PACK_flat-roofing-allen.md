## H2 — Why flat roofs fail in Collin County
A low-slope roof doesn't shed water — it *manages* it. When that management fails, it fails quietly:
- **Ponding.** Water standing more than 48 hours accelerates membrane breakdown and adds load.
- **Seam separation.** Heat cycling in North Texas summers works welds and laps loose.
- **Penetration flashing.** HVAC curbs, vents, and drains are where most leaks actually begin.
- **Hail bruising on membrane.** Impact fractures the reinforcement below the surface — invisible until it splits.

By the time a tenant reports a ceiling stain, water has usually traveled a long way from the entry point.

## H2 — Hail on a flat roof is a documentation problem
On shingles, hail leaves marks anyone can photograph. On TPO or modified bitumen, it leaves **bruises and micro-fractures** that require you to know exactly what you're looking at — and to photograph it in a way an adjuster accepts.
