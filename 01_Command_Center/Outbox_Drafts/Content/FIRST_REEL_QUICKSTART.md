# 🎬 Your First Reel — Two Ways, Both Foolproof

## 🅰️ WAY 1 — Cut up a real clip (Video Editor tab)
Best for: your brother on camera / real job footage.

1. Open **Local Studio → Video Editor** tab.
2. Click **Upload video** → pick `why pineapple roofing.MP4` (or any clip).
3. In the brief box, paste exactly:
   > *Cut to the strongest 20 seconds. Remove dead air, long pauses, and "umms." Burn bold 2-word captions. Keep everything else untouched.*
4. Click **Edit this video**. Wait for it to finish (watch the Agent Log).
5. When done, the final video shows in **Output** → download it.
6. **Add your end card** (last 3 seconds), text on screen:
   > 🍍 free roof inspection · (972) 928-0788 · RCAT #03-0637
7. Grab a caption from **Social_Hormozi_CaptionPack.md** (Post 1 = insurance hook).
8. **Blotato** → upload video + paste caption → select platforms → **Schedule** (don't publish) → tell Saia for the GO.

---

## 🅱️ WAY 2 — NotebookLM 60-sec Video (with CTA)
Best for: an explainer/authority short built from your own SOPs. No filming needed.

1. Open **NotebookLM → PM7 Brand & Content** (or SEO Playbook).
2. In the **Studio** panel (right side) → click **Video Overview**.
3. Click **Customize** and paste exactly:
   > Create a 60-second short video for DFW homeowners.
   > Structure (50/5/3): 0–5s hook — "Your roof looks fine. Your insurance company might disagree." 5–52s value — explain how hidden hail damage costs homeowners their claim, and how Pineapple Roofing documents every hit with photos the adjuster can't dismiss. Cite IKO Certified and RCAT License #03-0637, family-owned since 2021, 5-star rated. 52–60s call-to-action — "Call 972-928-0788 to book your free roof inspection."
   > Tone: confident, warm, trustworthy — not salesy.
   > BRAND LAW: never say "free" (always "free roof inspection / free roof inspection"), never "GAF" (always "IKO Certified"), never the color green.
4. Generate. When it's done, **download** it from the Studio panel.
5. Post it the same way — Blotato, scheduled, your GO.

## 🍍 Which to use?
- **Real family/job footage → Way 1** (Video Editor). More trust, more conversions.
- **No footage / want an authority explainer → Way 2** (NotebookLM). Fully AI, no filming.
- **Do both** — post the real reel Tuesday, the explainer Thursday.

**Every video ends the same:** 🍍 free roof inspection · (972) 928-0788 · RCAT #03-0637.
