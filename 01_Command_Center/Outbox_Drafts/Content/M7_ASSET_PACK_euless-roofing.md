The CPPA is Pineapple Roofing's documentation-first process for Euless properties. It is designed to give the owner a structured visual baseline before a proposal or major roofing decision.

The CPPA includes:
1. **Property-specific imagery** from accessible ground and roof perspectives.
2. **Component-level organization** so owners know what each image represents.
3. **A practical summary** of observed conditions, priorities, and possible next steps.
4. **A documented starting point** for homeowner, property-manager, or carrier conversations.
