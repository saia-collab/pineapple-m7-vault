## LAYER 2 — REVIEWS (the #1 driver of LSA ranking + trust)
More 5-star Google reviews = higher LSA rank = cheaper leads. Ask after EVERY completed job.

**Same-day text (after job done):**
```
Hi [Name], it was an honor protecting your home. Family to family — would you share a quick
Google review? It helps other North Texas neighbors find us: [GOOGLE_REVIEW_LINK]
Mālō ‘aupito. — [Your Name], Pineapple Contractors
```

**3-day nudge (if none yet):**
```
Hi [Name], no pressure at all — if you had a good experience, a 1-line Google review would
mean the world to our family business: [GOOGLE_REVIEW_LINK]. Thank you!
```

**Targets:** ask 100% of finished jobs · aim 5+ new reviews/week · reply to every review (good or bad).
**Get your link:** Google Business Profile → Ask for reviews → copy the short link → paste above.

## LAYER 3 — ATTRIBUTION (know what works, double down)
Simple beats fancy. One sheet tells you where to spend.

**Add to every intake:** "How did you hear about us?" (LSA / Google / Meta / Referral / Yard sign)
**Tag Meta links:** `?utm_source=meta&utm_campaign=storm&utm_content=local_fan`
**Track in one Google Sheet** (columns):
```
Date | Name | Source | Job $ | CPPA booked? | Job signed? | Review asked? | Review left?
```
**Weekly math (Monday):** for each source → leads, booked jobs, $ signed, cost per booked job.
Put next week's budget on the source with the lowest **cost per signed job** (not per click).
