# M7 GROWTH ENGINE — SEO/AEO · LEADS · BRANDING

How to grow Pineapple Contractors using the AI ecosystem you already built. The split:
**NotebookLM = facts/research. Gemini Gems = your brand voice. Hermes = schedules it.
brand_firewall = compliance. You = press publish.**

---

## THE GROWTH FLYWHEEL (everything feeds reviews + reviews feed everything)
`LSA + Ads → fast response → booked CPPA → great job → ASK FOR REVIEW → more reviews →
higher LSA/Maps rank → cheaper leads → repeat.` Reviews are the center. Guard them.

---

## 1. AEO / GEO — be the answer AI search gives (your edge over competitors)
Goal: when someone (or ChatGPT/Google AI) asks "best Frisco roofer for hail claims," you're cited.
- **NotebookLM (research):** load competitor pages + Google "People Also Ask" + your reviews.
  Prompt: *"Based ONLY on these sources, list the 15 questions Frisco homeowners ask about hail
  roof claims, and the gaps competitors don't answer well. Cite sources."*
- **Gemini Gem (write):** a "Pineapple Roofing Writer" Gem (PACT framework) turns each question into
  an FAQ answer in your voice — answer in the **first 40 words**, then detail.
- **Publish:** add as FAQ pages + FAQ schema on each service-area page (see M7_WEBSITE_SEO_FIXLIST.md).
- **Proof:** embed 2-3 real Google reviews per page (fresh, keyword-rich, trust).
> Why it works: AI engines cite pages that answer directly + have authority signals (your 200+ reviews).

## 2. LOCAL SEO (Maps + GBP)
- Fix NAP everywhere → 972-928-0788 (done in M7; update GBP/BBB/Yelp/FB).
- GBP: 2 posts + fresh photos/week from `02_Media_Vault`, seed Q&A, reply to every review.
- Service-area pages already exist — strengthen the top 5 cities (Frisco, McKinney, Plano, Lewisville, Allen) with unique copy + reviews + FAQ schema.

## 3. LEAD GENERATION (in priority order)
1. **LSA** (live) — respond <5 min, mark booked, dispute bad leads. Highest ROI today.
2. **Review loop** (Hermes `review_request_loop`) — compounds rank, lowers cost-per-lead.
3. **Meta campaign** — `Outbox_Drafts/2026-06-18_Campaign_Storm_Launch.md`, $250/wk, you launch.
4. **Content/AEO** — the FAQ + blog engine above captures "research" searchers.

## 4. BRANDING (your unfair advantage)
- The story sells: **Polynesian-owned, family-operated, since 2005, 200+ five-star reviews.** Lead with it.
- Founder/personal brand (Saia) — heritage + behind-the-scenes outperforms the company page. Cross-post.
- Consistency: 3-4 posts/week, rotate Sale / Culture / Recruit (asset library in the CRM).
- One look everywhere: Royal Navy + Pineapple Gold, never green. The firewall keeps it honest.

## 90-DAY PLAN (don't do it all at once)
**Weeks 1-2 — Foundation:** fix NAP + "free"→CPPA on site/GBP; turn on Hermes review loop; launch Local Fan ad set.
**Weeks 3-6 — Capture:** AEO FAQ pages (NotebookLM→Gemini), 2 blog posts, GBP posts/photos weekly, log every lead in CRM.
**Weeks 7-12 — Scale:** double down on the winning lead source (Attribution tab), expand top-5 city pages, weekly testimonial video from your 39GB media.

## THE ONLY METRIC THAT MATTERS
**Cost per booked CPPA → signed job, per source.** Everything above exists to lower it. Track it in the CRM Attribution tab; shift budget to the winner monthly.
