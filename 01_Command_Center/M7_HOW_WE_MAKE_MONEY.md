# 💰 M7 — HOW PINEAPPLE ACTUALLY MAKES MONEY
### The one page that turns all the tooling into cash. Read this when you feel lost.

## The thing you're missing (the honest answer)
You're not missing a tool. You've built a command center, a 19-step SOP, 8 loops, a Kanban, a CRM, an
Agent OS — that's *more* than most agencies have. **What's missing is the last inch: nothing has shipped
to a real customer yet.** Every asset is sitting **PAUSED in Outbox_Drafts**, waiting on one human move.
The machine is built and idling in the driveway. The money starts the second you **publish + answer leads.**

> Second thing: **don't copy Julian Goldie's business model.** He sells courses and a community — his
> dashboard monetizes *content → members*. You sell **roofs.** Your OS monetizes **content → local leads →
> signed jobs.** Same cockpit, different engine. Admire his dashboard; run *your* money path below.

## 🚀 THE 7-DAY GO-LIVE (turn the infrastructure into the first dollars)
You've built for weeks. This week you **ship.** One move a day.

- **Day 1 — Reviews on.** Take the staged review texts in `Outbox_Drafts/`, fill in 3 real recent
  customers, and **send them.** (Reviews are your #1 LSA-rank + trust lever.)
- **Day 2 — Social live.** Take the 8 staged Week-1 captions, post **3** today across FB/IG. Pin your
  best testimonial Reel.
- **Day 3 — Speed-to-lead locked.** Turn on LSA phone alerts, pin the instant text-back. Answer every
  lead in 5 min from now on. This single habit can double bookings.
- **Day 4 — SEO live.** Publish the 3 staged FAQ-schema pages to the site (or hand to whoever runs it).
- **Day 5 — Ad up.** Review the Storm Launch campaign, set Meta to $250/wk, launch the Local Fan ad set.
- **Day 6 — Fill the funnel.** Post 2 more captions + 2 GBP posts from the media. Ask the day's finished
  customers for reviews.
- **Day 7 — Read the scoreboard.** Open the weekly review brief: which source booked a job? Put next
  week's effort there. Repeat.

After 7 days you're not "building an AI system" anymore — you're **running a lead machine.**

## 📅 THE STEADY-STATE RHYTHM (after go-live)
**Daily reflexes (non-negotiable, ~20 min total):**
1. Answer every lead in **5 minutes.**
2. Text every finished customer for a **Google review.**
3. Do **one** hero action (post, publish, or close).

**Weekly (Monday, 30 min):** open the Command Center → read the weekly review → approve the week's staged
drafts → publish the winners → move budget to whatever signed a job.

**The AI runs in the background** (the 8 loops draft everything PAUSED). **You just approve and ship.**

## 🧭 RUNNING IT SMOOTH (VP notes)
- **Don't let the machine run you.** When Hermes stalls or the disk fills, that's a pit stop, not a
  crisis — the leads still get answered by you. Fix tools between revenue moves, not instead of them.
- **One screen:** keep `M7_COMMAND_CENTER.html` open in a browser tab beside Obsidian. That's your cockpit.
- **One source of truth:** the vault. One Google account (business) owns it. Media lives off the local disk.
- **One rule above all:** **Outbox Shield** — agents draft, Saia ships and spends. That's what keeps a
  fast machine from making expensive mistakes.
- **Measure the only number that matters:** cost per **signed job** (not clicks, not likes). The weekly
  review tracks it. Everything else is vanity.

> You built the hard part. The business now is simple: keep the funnel full, answer fast, ask for reviews,
> ship one thing a day. Plan — Push — Play — Profit.
