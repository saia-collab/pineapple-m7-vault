## 🟢 TIER 1 — Google Business Profile (FREE · do this week · biggest payoff)

This is your #1 lead source. A homeowner searches "roofer near me" → Google shows the map → you want to be in the top 3. Here's how you get there:

- [ ] **Primary category:** "Roofing Contractor" (exact)

- [ ] **Description (750 characters, keyword-rich):** mention Frisco/DFW, storm & hail, IKO Certified, RCAT #03-0637, since 2005, CPPA. *(I can write this for you — just ask.)*
- [ ] **Services list:** Roof Replacement, Storm/Hail Repair, Leak Repair, Commercial Roofing, Restoration, Insurance Claim Help
- [ ] **Service areas:** Frisco, Lewisville, Plano, McKinney, Allen, Denton, Grapevine, Euless
- [ ] **Hours + phone (972-928-0788) + website** correct on BOTH profiles (Roofing + Restoration)

### B. Reviews — the single most powerful lever (ongoing, weekly)
- [ ] **Ask EVERY happy customer** the day the job finishes. Text them the review link.
- [ ] **Goal: 10+ new reviews per month.** Velocity matters more than total.
- [ ] **Reply to every review** — good AND bad — and **name the neighborhood** in your reply ("Thanks for trusting us with your Starwood roof!"). *(I draft these for you — never sound like a robot.)*

### C. Posts — keep the profile "alive" (weekly, 5 min)
- [ ] **1 GBP post per week** on BOTH profiles. Use the ones I already wrote (storm/CPPA). Active profiles rank higher.

### D. Photos — proof sells (weekly, 5 min)

## 🔵 TIER 3 — LSA / Local Services Ads (PAID · do AFTER Tier 1 is solid)

LSA = the ads at the very top with the ✅ "Google Guaranteed" badge. You only pay per lead. **Don't start until reviews + GBP are strong** (LSA uses them to rank you).

- [ ] **Apply for "Google Guaranteed"** — Google runs a background + license check. You have RCAT #03-0637, so you qualify. This badge IS the advantage.
- [ ] **Set a weekly budget** you're comfortable with (start small).
- [ ] **Reply to every LSA lead in under 5 minutes** — LSA ranks you on response speed. *(Your speed-to-lead auto-text handles this.)*
- [ ] **Dispute bad leads** (wrong area, spam) — Google refunds them. Keeps your cost per real lead low.
