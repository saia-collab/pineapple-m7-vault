Hail damage on a Frisco, TX roof is usually invisible from the ground — and documentation is what wins the insurance claim.

Hail does not always break a shingle. It bruises it, fracturing the mat layer beneath the granules that actually keeps water out. From the driveway the roof looks perfect; under a camera at six feet it does not.

Pineapple Roofing documents the whole roof — drone and ground, slope by slope — photographing every impact plus the collateral dents on gutters, vents, and flashing that corroborate the storm date. The homeowner keeps that evidence file whether or not they hire us. Evidence beats opinions.
