# When to use /adhd (and when NOT to)

You have ADHD. This skill is powerful but it can cut both ways. Read this once.

## ✅ Use `/adhd` when you are STUCK CHOOSING
A real fork with 2+ real options and you're frozen. Examples:
- `/adhd "3 ways to follow up with a storm lead without sounding like AI"`
- `/adhd "how should I price a premium estate reroof vs a standard one"`
It hands you ONE starred pick + a 5-minute first step. That's the point: it ends the freeze.

## 🛑 Do NOT use `/adhd` when you already know the next action
If the task is "reply to this lead" or "post this photo" — you don't need 30 ideas.
30 ideas is the ADHD trap, not the cure. More options = more freeze.
For those, type the task with the word **`just`** in front and I answer in one line:
- `just draft the lead text-back for this name`

## The real ADHD medicine here
Your struggle isn't a shortage of ideas — you have a garage full of tools already.
Your struggle is STARTING and FINISHING one thing. For that:
1. **One task, visible, right now.** Not a list. One.
2. **Let me hold the list**, not your head. Ask me "what's my one next thing?"
3. **Tiny first step.** "Open the leads screen" beats "do all my SEO."
4. **Tell me when it's done.** Naming the win closes the loop your brain needs closed.

> When in doubt, don't open a new tool. Ask me: **"what's my ONE next thing?"**
